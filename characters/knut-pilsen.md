**Knut Pilsen** is a resident of [Sire](/places/sire) who was rescued during the [Burning of Sire](/events/burning-of-sire). [Varis](/characters/varis) and [Fenris](/characters/fenris) later escorted him and [Anne](/characters/anne-knutsen) toward the capital ([Return to Morgan's Keep](/events/return-to-morgans-keep)).

## Role in the Story

During the devastating fire that consumed [Sire](/places/sire), Knut's daughter [Anne](/characters/anne-knutsen) came running to [Fenris](/characters/fenris) and [Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok), screaming that her father was trapped and would burn to death. The two heroes rushed to find Knut pinned under a fallen, burning tree near the riverbank, his hair on fire.

[Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) smothered the flames with her cloak while [Fenris](/characters/fenris) fetched water from the river using magic. Fenris then transformed into a bear to lift the tree, and together they carried Knut to safety at the river's edge. Fenris used healing magic to stabilize him.

Once revived, Knut called out for **Betty** — his horse, which had fled across the river. Anne had climbed onto the party's horse during the rescue.

On the road to [Morgan's Keep](/places/morgans-keep), Knut agreed to sell Betty to the party for thirty-five gold, giving him and [Anne](/characters/anne-knutsen) money to begin again in the city. The next morning, [Varis](/characters/varis) offered him work carrying messages and goods, tending the horses, and cooking. Knut asked for a day or two to decide ([Session 24](/sessions/session-24)).

Ahead of selling farm cheese into [Morgan's Keep](/places/morgans-keep), the party placed money and a hidden tracking compass among Knut's freight ([Session 25](/sessions/session-25)). Before a later delivery, they agreed to explain the compass openly as a way to keep the courier safe ([Session 28](/sessions/session-28)).
