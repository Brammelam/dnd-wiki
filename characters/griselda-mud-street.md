**Griselda** is a tough dwarf with a **broken arm** who offered [Varis](/characters/varis), [Flint](/characters/flint), and [Peren](/characters/peren) work **intimidating delinquent tenants** on Mud Street after they refused an assassination job from [Salander](/characters/salander). She connected [Peren](/characters/peren) toward poison contacts.

During the [debt-collection fight](/events/rinden-mud-street-brawl), she warned against unnecessary killing, but [Flint](/characters/flint) killed one of the defenders. The survivors paid the rent. After bargaining, Griselda paid the party fifteen gold each and told them about a retired poison expert living by the sea on the road to [Dior](/places/dior) ([Session 7](/sessions/session-07)). That lead brought them to [Thorn](/characters/thorn-alchemist).
