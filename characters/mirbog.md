**Mirbog** is a half-orc portal guardian sent by Titania to keep a horde of dead sealed away. [Elowen](/characters/elowen) met the warden inside a crystal chamber, where Mirbog had been trapped as a sacrifice and endured a long isolation ([Session 12](/sessions/session-12)).

They performed a ritual to escape. Before departing for the nearest town, Mirbog gave the party a formula to reseal the portal and accepted provisions. The party considered returning the relics that sustained the ward but continued toward [Fort John](/places/fort-john).

The wiki's established inventory places Blade of the Ethereal with [Fenris](/characters/fenris), and Sprout and Nathair's Mask with [Elowen](/characters/elowen), during the [lake hydra delve](/events/lake-hydra-hunt). The source recounts the mask choice in this encounter; the chronology discrepancy is noted in [Session 12](/sessions/session-12), without recording a second award.
