**Elowen** is a Wood Elf Warlock played by **Vanja**. She has a pact with a mysterious patron whose calling she first felt near the [Azure Oak](/places/azure-oak-grove).

## Background

Little is known about Elowen's background before joining the party. She is a Wood Elf with a connection to otherworldly powers through her warlock pact. Her patron's nature became partially apparent during the [Azure Oak quest](/events/azure-oak-quest) near [Turn](/places/turn).

## Relics & gear

At the [Lake Hydra Hunt](/events/lake-hydra-hunt) delve **three relic-crystals** clamped **an undeath-aligned portal**; voices within warned against carrying **everything** away. **She** still departed with **two** tied rewards (**Sprout**, **Nathair's Mask**) while **[Fenris](/characters/fenris)** claimed **Blade of the Ethereal** ([Session 11](/sessions/session-11)):

- **Sprout** — Buckler (**+1 AC**). When using **an attack of opportunity**, may **cast a spell** instead of making the OA attack; when **successfully blocking** an attack, may **cast a spell** as a **Reaction**.
- **Nathair's Mask** — **+2** bonus to illusion spells (**spell attack rolls and save DC**, per adjudication); **once per dawn**, may cast **_Nathair's Mischief_** **without expending** a spell slot.

## Adventures

### Azure Oak Quest (Session 3)
Near [Turn](/places/turn), Elowen felt her patron calling while the party searched for the [Azure Oak](/places/azure-oak-grove).

### Selling the Seed (Session 4)
In [Rinden](/places/rinden), Elowen negotiated the sale of the Azure Oak seed in the [Saltside](/places/saltside) district, ultimately selling it for **120 gold** to a buyer at a fancy club — turning down an offer from [The Academy](/factions/the-academy) for services instead of gold.

### The Fisherman and the Dream (Session 8)
While [Varis](/characters/varis) investigated their pursuers under an invisibility spell, Elowen sought shelter at a fisherman's home. She blasted open the door when he refused entry, stopped his son from escaping, and charmed the father. They later left money for the damage. During her next rest, [Sommerhimmel](/characters/sommerhimmel) appeared as Titania's messenger and explained that her powers carried an obligation to perform a future task ([Session 8](/sessions/session-08)).

### Thorn's Tower (Session 9)
With [Varis](/characters/varis) and [Peren](/characters/peren), she trained under [Thorn](/characters/thorn-alchemist) and went to harvest serpent venom. When Thorn **drugged the oars** so others would faint and leave Peren alone against the snake, Elowen **destroyed Thorn's front door** in a rage ([Thorn's Serpent and Fey Visions](/events/thorn-serpent-and-visions)).

### Hooves Bay & the Outpost (Session 10)
At [Hooves Bay](/places/hooves-bay), [Sommerhimmel](/characters/sommerhimmel) was angry at Elowen for lying to the fey and directed her to seek a hidden treasure in a grove. Elowen found a secret phrase that opened an ancient elven military outpost, where she had a vision of a masked woman who had once protected the place.

### Fey Portal (Session 10)
When leaving the [Fey Realm](/places/fey-realm), [Flint](/characters/flint) reached back through a closing portal to grab his hammer. The portal [severed his arm at the elbow](/events/fey-portal) — a traumatic moment for the entire party.

### Lake Hydra & crystal wards (Session 11)
Elowen left the hydra lair gripping **Sprout** and **Nathair's Mask**—two thirds of the crystal triumvirate—after **[Fenris](/characters/fenris)** already secured **Blade of the Ethereal**, together flouting trapped **orc** counsel about sparing the undeath-aligned gate ([Lake Hydra Hunt](/events/lake-hydra-hunt)).

### Taken from Fort John (Session 12)
At [Fort John](/places/fort-john), Elowen sent her fish familiar into the fort's well and used its position to direct *Stinking Cloud* into the courtyard. During the party's infiltration, [Sommerhimmel](/characters/sommerhimmel) appeared through a portal, confronted her over the removal of all three relics, and took her away. Her companions lost contact with her ([Session 12](/sessions/session-12)).

## Relationships

- **[Thorn](/characters/thorn-alchemist)** — Poison tutor; Elowen smashed his door after the oar trick
- **[Sommerhimmel](/characters/sommerhimmel)** — A fey entity who has both punished and guided Elowen
- **[Fenris](/characters/fenris)** — Shared **Lake Hydra** crystal fallout: he holds **Blade of the Ethereal**; **she** bears **Sprout** + **Nathair's Mask** ([Lake Hydra Hunt](/events/lake-hydra-hunt)).
- **Her Patron** — The source of her warlock powers; details remain mysterious
