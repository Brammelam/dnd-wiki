**Salander** is the leader of [The Crabs](/factions/the-crabs), a criminal gang operating in [Rinden](/places/rinden).

## Role in the Story

[Peren](/characters/peren) and [Varis](/characters/varis) sought out The Crabs to gain information about who framed them for the [Crown Prince's murder](/events/crown-prince-murder). After proving themselves by watching fight pits and rescuing a Crabs member, they earned a meeting with Salander.

Salander offered a deal: he would **dig into who framed the party** if they did a job for him first — exposing the shady business dealings of [Father Terry](/characters/father-terry).

After the [Scully Stronghold infiltration](/events/scully-stronghold-infiltration), the party brought him a sample of sourleaf smuggled beneath wine. He identified its northern origin and paid each party member five gold. He said their framing involved someone connected to the palace guard, directed them toward [Helga](/characters/helga) at her daughter's wedding in [Dior](/places/dior), and offered [Bob Swords](/characters/bob-swords) as a local contact ([Session 6](/sessions/session-06)).

In [Session 7](/sessions/session-07), Salander doubted that [Helga](/characters/helga) had acted alone and said he could find no noble called "Countess Barros." He offered more information in exchange for killing a city guard who obstructed his interests. The party refused the assassination job.

See: [The Crabs Job](/events/the-crabs-job)
