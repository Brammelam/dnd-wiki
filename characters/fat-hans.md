**Fat Hans** is a former guard turned mercenary who led a squad to ambush the party on the road.

## Role in the Story

After a cart crash, Fat Hans and his mercenaries caught up to the party. When interrogated after their defeat, he claimed that some guards killed during the [escape from Morgan's Keep](/events/escape-from-morgans-keep) were sons of a countess who had hired him to exact revenge.

The party defeated the mercenaries in combat, stole a horse, a compass, and gear. They tied Hans and his surviving men to a tree before moving on.

His employer's identity remained uncertain: [Salander](/characters/salander) later reported that he could find no noble called "Countess Barros" ([Session 7](/sessions/session-07)).

See: [Mercenary Ambush](/events/mercenary-ambush)
