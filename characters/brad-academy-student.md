**Brad** is a polite, unremarkable mage the party used as social cover to reach [Scully Stronghold](/places/scully-stronghold). [Elowen](/characters/elowen) posed as his guest after a fabricated tale of searching for a lost love on the island.

During that visit, Brad told [Lady Amber](/characters/lady-amber) that [the Academy](/factions/the-academy) had found clues to her missing sister. He requested 200 gold for further investigation, and she instructed her treasurer to pay him ([Session 6](/sessions/session-06)).

He later hired [Varis](/characters/varis) and [Elowen](/characters/elowen) to courier a locked metal box to [Toltec](/characters/toltec) in [Dior](/places/dior), paying forty gold in advance and promising another forty on delivery ([Session 8](/sessions/session-08)).

Brad would not reveal the box's contents, but assured them it was harmless and claimed divination had shown it reaching [Dior](/places/dior) safely in their hands. He also explained that [Varis](/characters/varis)'s fairy pollen was an enchantment reagent requiring a skilled mage to use.
