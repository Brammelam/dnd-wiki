**Jamaris Jamarisen Llamar** joined [Varis](/characters/varis), [Peren](/characters/peren), [Fenris](/characters/fenris), and [Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) during the [Capital Cathedral Undead Riot](/events/capital-cathedral-undead-riot). He struck a large undead creature with the butt of his spear, knocking it back onto another attacker and buying the group time.

Later he shouted "Stop!" with assumed authority as the cathedral's lights failed and the undead briefly froze. His attempt to command the guards was less successful: only one obeyed. After the escape, he introduced himself in the tavern and said his faith had brought him to the party. The notes render his name inconsistently as "Jamar Jamarsen Lamar" during that conversation ([Session 26](/sessions/session-26)).

## Highlights

- [Session 26](/sessions/session-26) — Helped hold the cathedral attackers back, then joined the party's preparations to infiltrate the [Humanus Association](/factions/humanus-association).
- During the subsequent street ambush, his attempt to distract a man bound for [Lady Alabaster](/characters/lady-alabaster)'s house by pretending to be drunk failed. The target fled before [Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) knocked him unconscious.
