**Varis** is a Half-Elf Fighter played by **Bram**. A veteran of the Morganian army, Varis served as a swordsman before developing farsightedness that forced him to retrain as an archer. He has since become a formidable samurai-style bowman.

## Background

Varis served in the Morganian army as a swordsman. Over time, his vision deteriorated — he became farsighted, making close-quarters combat increasingly difficult. Rather than retire, he adapted, retraining himself as an archer where his long-distance vision became an asset rather than a liability.

He was living in [Morgan's Keep](/places/morgans-keep) when he was arrested alongside other party members for the [murder of the Crown Prince](/events/crown-prince-murder).

## Notable Equipment

- **Longbow** — His primary weapon. In [Session 16](/sessions/session-16), he left a damaged bow with [Tore Idle](/characters/tore-idle) in [Dior](/places/dior) for a fifty-gold repair and upgrade, choosing greater power and range at the cost of aiming difficulty.
- **Counterattack sword** — Identified by [Rudolf the Great](/characters/rudolf-the-great) in [Session 15](/sessions/session-15); allowed a parry and counterattack when an enemy missed. Varis attuned to it that day.
- **Quiver** — Holds thirty arrows; bought from [Bob Swords](/characters/bob-swords) in [Session 15](/sessions/session-15).
- **Rapier** — Secondary melee weapon
- **Glasses** — Rare optical aids from [Alverike](/places/alverike); he sold a pair to [Lynn Salt](/characters/lynn-salt) for 300 gold
- **City Watch insignia** — A badge from his time in the military, used to gain trust
- **Amulet of the Living** — **Random/session loot from the hydra-stronghold delve** (**not one of the crystal-trio relics**). **Three** charges that unleash effects akin to **_Turn Undead_**; **two charges remain** after the [Crypt of Alabaster](/places/crypt-of-alabaster) breach ([Session 25](/sessions/session-25)).

## Key Abilities

- **Fighting Spirit** — Grants temporary hit points and advantage on attacks
- **Sharpshooter** — Deadly precision with ranged attacks at the cost of accuracy
- **Second Wind** — Quick self-healing in combat

## Adventures

### The Escape (Session 1)
Varis was among the first arrested at [Morgan's Keep](/places/morgans-keep). He fought alongside [Flint](/characters/flint), [Peren](/characters/peren), and [Thia](/characters/thia) to [escape](/events/escape-from-morgans-keep) the capital and flee toward [Rinden](/places/rinden).

### Dior & the Crypt (Sessions 16–18)
In [Dior](/places/dior), Varis negotiated the sale of magical glasses at [the Academy](/places/the-academy), explored shops with [Bob Swords](/characters/bob-swords), and commissioned a bow repair from [Tore Idle](/characters/tore-idle). He then ventured into [the Crypt](/places/the-crypt) through an entrance supplied by [Carlos](/characters/carlos), where the party fought a fire elemental and encountered a dragon. A flute summoned water spirits that briefly drove the elemental into the stone before combat resumed. The [Session 17](/sessions/session-17) notes disagree over whether Varis or [Fenris](/characters/fenris) played during that part of the fight.

### Helga Confrontation (Sessions 20–21)
Varis shot out [Helga](/characters/helga)'s lantern at the [Tyr Moir](/places/tyr-moir) graveyard and rappelled from a rooftop in [Session 20](/sessions/session-20). He took the lead in questioning her about the [Crown Prince murder](/events/crown-prince-murder) as the meeting continued in [Session 21](/sessions/session-21).

### Journey to Morgan's Keep (Session 23)
Traveling with [Peren](/characters/peren), Varis survived the [Road Inn Ambush](/events/road-inn-ambush). [Thorn](/characters/thorn-alchemist) alone attacked Peren in the stable; Varis joined the fight, then gave Thorn a **healing potion** to **stabilize** him. He met [Lynn Salt](/characters/lynn-salt) and [Thor](/characters/thor-veteran), sharing stories over cheese before continuing toward the capital.

### Return to Morgan's Keep (Session 24)
With [Fenris](/characters/fenris), Varis escorted [Knut Pilsen](/characters/knut-pilsen) and [Anne Knutsen](/characters/anne-knutsen). They agreed to buy Knut's horse Betty for thirty-five gold to give the family a start in the city. They also pursued intelligence on [Lieutenant Jet](/characters/lieutenant-jet) and the [Crypt of Alabaster](/places/crypt-of-alabaster) toward clearing the [Crown Prince murder](/events/crown-prince-murder) charge ([Return to Morgan's Keep](/events/return-to-morgans-keep)).

### Crypt of Morgan (Session 25)
Within the capital cathedral's undercrypts Varis stirred the wrong **Gregory Alabaster** tomb before pinning down **the Fourth**. He grappled **sapphire orb** tethering hostility—experimented verbally and physically—and **splintered it without ending the siege**. Spending **Amulet of the Living** charge expelled undead bottleneck while allies escaped gale corridor tactics ([Session 25](/sessions/session-25)).

### Cathedral riot & Humanus stakeout (Session 26)
After the orb broke, Varis carried [Peren](/characters/peren) up the crypt stairs and used the Amulet of the Living to ward off the undead. He shot a pursuing corpse, helped secure the exit, and gave Peren another potion. In the nave, the crowd believed a fallen armored figure was the murdered [Crown Prince](/events/crown-prince-murder); that identification remained unconfirmed. After escaping with [Thor](/characters/thor-veteran), the party planned to investigate the [Humanus Association](/factions/humanus-association) and [Lady Alabaster](/characters/lady-alabaster). They abducted a finely dressed man approaching her house, identified in [Session 27](/sessions/session-27) as [Lieutenant Preben Jet](/characters/lieutenant-jet) ([Capital Cathedral Undead Riot](/events/capital-cathedral-undead-riot)).

### Fall of Lady Alabaster (Sessions 27–29)
Varis infiltrated the [Humanus](/factions/humanus-association) cellar in disguise and believed one of its masked members was [Lady Alabaster](/characters/lady-alabaster). He later boarded her departing ship, shattered her smoke crystal with an arrow, helped evacuate its prisoners, and brought her before the King's Council. When [Viktor Carnelian](/characters/viktor-carnelian) attacked the hearing, Varis struck him with an ice arrow ([Fall of Lady Alabaster](/events/fall-of-lady-alabaster)).
