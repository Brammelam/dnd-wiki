**Konstantin Carnelian** hunted the party over a murder accusation on behalf of [Lady Alabaster](/characters/lady-alabaster).

## Pursuit of the party

In his first confrontation with the party, in [Rinden](/places/rinden), Konstantin challenged [Varis](/characters/varis), [Flint](/characters/flint), and [Peren](/characters/peren) over a murder charge. He brought Flint and Peren down before enforcers from [the Crabs](/factions/the-crabs) forced him to retreat and carried the party to safety ([Session 7](/sessions/session-07)).

[Markus](/characters/markus-lieutenant) helped the party avoid him during the endgame of the [Fort John Rebellion](/events/fort-john-rebellion). [Session 15](/sessions/session-15) resumed with Konstantin defeated; the available notes do not recount that fight. He told [Varis](/characters/varis) and [Fenris](/characters/fenris) that his employer sought them over her son Tom's death and insisted he had intended to capture them. They seized his tracking compass and accepted payment for enough healing to let him leave. He promised to end the pursuit.
