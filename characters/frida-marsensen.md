**Frida Marsensen** is [Karl Marsensen](/characters/karl-marsensen)'s wife and a leader among the rebels during the [Fort John Rebellion](/events/fort-john-rebellion).

She welcomed [Varis](/characters/varis) and [Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) after their staged defense of the fort, believing the rebels could survive a week on the baron's beer despite their unusable well ([Session 12](/sessions/session-12)). After [Fenris](/characters/fenris)'s tower escape, she recognized signs of a druid and ordered additional guards ([Session 13](/sessions/session-13)).

Frida sought help from [Dior](/places/dior) and initially failed to reach an agreement with [Baron Peridott](/characters/baron-peridott). When thirsty townspeople began deserting, she negotiated the fort's surrender in exchange for limited rations ([Session 14](/sessions/session-14)).
