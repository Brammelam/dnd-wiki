**Gregory Alabaster the Fourth** was a member of the [Humanus Association](/factions/humanus-association)'s inner circle, recently buried in the [Crypt of Alabaster](/places/crypt-of-alabaster). The intelligence service directed the party to his remains for information about the association's next meeting ([Session 24](/sessions/session-24)).

## Questioning in the crypt

After mistakenly disturbing Gregory Alabaster the First's tomb, the party found Gregory the Fourth among fresh flowers. Blue light shone through his torso, matching the eyes of the undead pursuing them. [Varis](/characters/varis) used a questioning ring while [Peren](/characters/peren) held the doorway ([Session 25](/sessions/session-25)).

Gregory could not stop the undead. He explained that the [Humanus Association](/factions/humanus-association) changed meeting places each time: the last gathering had been at [Lieutenant Jet](/characters/lieutenant-jet)'s premises, and the next would be in [Lady Alabaster](/characters/lady-alabaster)'s wine cellar in two hours. Asked how to escape, he answered, "Run."

[Varis](/characters/varis) pulled a blue orb from Gregory's body, tried unsuccessfully to command it, and smashed it. The undead kept coming. The party escaped into the [Capital Cathedral Undead Riot](/events/capital-cathedral-undead-riot), still unable to explain the orb or who had controlled it.

## The association's account

At the meeting in [Session 27](/sessions/session-27), members of the [Humanus Association](/factions/humanus-association) said Gregory had returned changed from a journey. They debated whether the dangerous influence came from his own magic or something he had brought home, and discussed exploiting fear of the cathedral undead for political gain. Their claims did not establish the cause of his condition.

## Lineage distinctions

- **Gregory I** — the earlier ancestor whose tomb the party disturbed by mistake.
- **Gregory IV** — the recently deceased association member they intended to question.
