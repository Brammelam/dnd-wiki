**Lady Alabaster**, called **Gryvinda Alabaster** in the Humanus investigation, employed **[Konstantin Carnelian](/characters/konstantin-carnelian)** to pursue the party over the death of her son **Tom**.

The party identified her behind a mask at a [Humanus Association](/factions/humanus-association) meeting, then intercepted her aboard a ship carrying imprisoned elves. They rescued the captives and brought Alabaster before the King's Council. Under the cardinal's truth ritual, she admitted arranging the false accusations against the party and named [Old Garreth](/factions/toll-guild) as the person who ordered her. [Viktor Carnelian](/characters/viktor-carnelian) attacked before the council could learn more.
