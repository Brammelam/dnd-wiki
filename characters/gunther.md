**Gunther** is a large, heavily armored dwarf who accompanied [General Kamp](/factions/third-army) and [Molly Alabaster](/characters/molly-alabaster) in the King's Council at [Morgan's Keep](/places/morgans-keep).

## Role in the Story

Gunther raised his shield to protect [General Kamp](/factions/third-army) from [Fenris](/characters/fenris)'s elementals. He smashed the ice elemental, which burst in a cloud of frost, and left the chamber with the general ([Session 30](/sessions/session-30)).
