**Thor** is a veteran and intelligence contact. Before the party left [Fort John](/places/fort-john), a lieutenant advised [Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) to seek him at the Empty Barrel in [Morgan's Keep](/places/morgans-keep) ([Session 14](/sessions/session-14)). [Varis](/characters/varis) also remembered him as an old military contact while planning the return to the capital ([Session 21](/sessions/session-21)).

His first recorded encounter with the party was on the road toward [Morgan's Keep](/places/morgans-keep), where he shared cheese and rumors of Fortrejon and Kopp with [Varis](/characters/varis) and [Peren](/characters/peren) ([Session 23](/sessions/session-23)).

In the capital outskirts ([Session 24](/sessions/session-24)), [Varis](/characters/varis) found him changed — **battle-worn**, with a **staff** and **bad leg**. Thor's vouch mattered to a **spymaster** offering help. He returned with his superior **[Magda](/characters/magda)** — intelligence for [General Kamp](/factions/third-army), not an artificer.

During **[Session 26](/sessions/session-26)** he **opened a discreet coach**, sealed the fugitives inside, and wheeled them toward **[Lady Alabaster](/characters/lady-alabaster)**'s grounds while city panic still thrashed behind.

In [Sessions 27–28](/events/fall-of-lady-alabaster), Thor supplied the disguise, plans, transport, and surveillance used to infiltrate Humanus and locate Lady Alabaster. His allies could secure a hearing before the King's Council, but the party had to make the arrest themselves.
