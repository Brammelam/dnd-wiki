**Molly Alabaster** accompanied [General Kamp](/factions/third-army) during the fight in the King's Council at [Morgan's Keep](/places/morgans-keep).

## Role in the Story

After an attempt to stop [Fenris](/characters/fenris)'s magic, Molly stayed near the general and [Gunther](/characters/gunther). As they withdrew, she took an amulet hanging on the wall in the king's room, put it on, and left the chamber ([Session 30](/sessions/session-30)).

The stolen amulet was a separate item from the [Amulet of the Living](/characters/varis#notable-equipment), which remained with [Varis](/characters/varis). Its properties remain unknown.
