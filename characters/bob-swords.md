**Bob Swords** is a jovial Hobbit weapon merchant in [Dior](/places/dior). Despite his small stature, he deals in powerful magical weapons and armor, and has become a reliable ally and information source for the party.

## Inventory

Bob's shop features a variety of magical and mundane equipment:

- **Frost Leather Vest** — Armor from the cold north that protects the wearer and deals cold damage to attackers, but risks harming the wearer as well
- **Standard Leather Vest** — Better protection without magical side effects, at a lower cost
- **Runed Armor** — Expensive armor with magical runes providing 30 seconds of damage protection, with severe backlash afterward
- **Elemental Arrows** — Fire, ice, and lightning arrows (100 gold per arrow)
- **Grappling Hook** — Standard climbing equipment

Bob confirmed that magical armor would **adapt to a shapeshifter's form**, easing [Fenris](/characters/fenris)'s concerns about Wild Shape compatibility.

## Role in the Story

### Arrival in Dior — Session 15

Bob welcomed [Varis](/characters/varis) and [Fenris](/characters/fenris) to his shop and confirmed that he knew [Salander](/characters/salander). He explained that [Helga](/characters/helga)'s son was marrying one of the Nine Bishops and suggested using the ceremony's distractions to approach her. He offered information and contacts, while declining to intervene directly.

Bob bought [Fenris](/characters/fenris)'s shield and scimitar and sold [Varis](/characters/varis) regular and enhanced arrows with a thirty-arrow quiver. He promised to show them affordable magical armor the next morning.

### The Crypt and Wedding — Sessions 16–21

Bob referred the party to [Tore Idle](/characters/tore-idle) for the damaged bow and [Carlos](/characters/carlos) for unofficial treasure-hunting work. He later sold elemental arrows and a grappling hook and promised to research the temple's rooms, wall thickness, and drainage.

He obtained wedding invitations in [Session 20](/sessions/session-20). After the meeting with [Helga](/characters/helga), he bought them back at a reduced price and took the rented clothes in [Session 21](/sessions/session-21).
