**Viktor Carnelian** attended the King's Council hearing for [Lady Alabaster](/characters/lady-alabaster). When Alabaster named [Old Garreth](/factions/toll-guild) as the person behind the party's framing, Viktor tried to spear her before she could say more. The cardinal blocked the first thrust and was critically wounded in the struggle. Viktor then fought [Varis](/characters/varis), [Peren](/characters/peren), [Fenris](/characters/fenris), and [Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) as royal guards approached.

His exact relationship to [Konstantin Carnelian](/characters/konstantin-carnelian) and Old Garreth has not yet been established.
