**Viktor Carnelian** attended the King's Council hearing for [Lady Alabaster](/characters/lady-alabaster). When Alabaster named [Old Garreth](/factions/toll-guild) as the person behind the party's framing, Viktor tried to spear her before she could say more. The cardinal blocked the first thrust and was critically wounded in the struggle. Viktor then fought [Varis](/characters/varis), [Peren](/characters/peren), [Fenris](/characters/fenris), and [Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) as royal guards approached.

[Session 30](/sessions/session-30) established that [Konstantin Carnelian](/characters/konstantin-carnelian) is his brother. Viktor defended [Old Garreth](/factions/toll-guild), using his returning spear and shield against the party and the cardinal's dwarf warrior. His glowing armor repeatedly slowed his bleeding and helped him recover. [Varis](/characters/varis)'s magical arrows made him drop his shield and then fall unconscious, but he rose again and left the chamber. [Peren](/characters/peren) followed him into the corridor.
