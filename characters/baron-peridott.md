**Baron Peridott** hired the party as **independent agents** during the [Fort John Rebellion](/events/fort-john-rebellion), offering pay to extract **[Karl Marsensen](/characters/karl-marsensen)** or resolve the siege. **[Markus](/characters/markus-lieutenant)** served among his officers.

His initial offer was five gold per party member for extracting [Karl](/characters/karl-marsensen), or fifteen each for resolving the whole crisis. One of his family's bows was also raised as a possible reward ([Session 12](/sessions/session-12)).
