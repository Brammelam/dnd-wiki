The **Glassmaker** traded with [Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok), exchanging iron gear for a **glass axe** and glasses while she was separated from the party before the [lake hydra hunt](/events/lake-hydra-hunt).

The meeting took place in a glass-domed house. The Glassmaker summoned [Sommerhimmel](/characters/sommerhimmel) to guide [Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) back to her companions. The axe shattered while severing a hydra head, but its blade regrew at sunset. The glasses did not improve [Varis](/characters/varis)'s sight when he tried them ([Session 11](/sessions/session-11)).
