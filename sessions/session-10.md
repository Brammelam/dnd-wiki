## Recap

### The closing portal

After the party left the [Fey Realm](/places/fey-realm), [Fenris](/characters/fenris) took the others aside to tell them that [Flint](/characters/flint)'s injured leg was looking worse. While they talked, [Flint](/characters/flint) rose and returned to the portal. [Fenris](/characters/fenris) ran after him, turning into a cat, but could not reach him in time. [Flint](/characters/flint) reached through for his hammer, and the portal closed on his arm, severing it at the elbow.

### Passing Hooves Bay

The others continued toward [Hooves Bay](/places/hooves-bay). A boy asked them to protect the town from pirates. They investigated, but when they learned that the town guards had made no preparations to defend it, they chose to leave the settlement to handle the threat itself.

### The hidden outpost

That night, [Sommerhimmel](/characters/sommerhimmel) visited [Elowen](/characters/elowen), angry that she had lied to the fey. The messenger directed her to hidden treasure in a grove beside the road. In the morning, the party found the grove and spoke a secret phrase to open an ancient elven military outpost.

Inside, they found loot, met a ghost, and discovered a sealed portal to [Dead Isle](/places/dead-isle). Crystals stood beside an underground lake and appeared to protect the outpost. The party decided to take the largest one. When [Elowen](/characters/elowen) touched it, she saw a masked woman who had saved the place before. At the same moment, something moved in the lake.
