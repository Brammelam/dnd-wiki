## Recap

### Gal'Rok Rejoins the Party

[Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) tried the buttons outside the sealed crypt door without success and settled down to wait. After a while the door opened. She passed the opened coffins and crossed the rope above the ravine, nearly setting herself alight with her torch.

A bridge began collapsing as [Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) approached the central platform, but she ran clear. Beyond it, the dragon emerged, [Fenris](/characters/fenris) lay burned and unconscious, and [Varis](/characters/varis) fled across another bridge. The dragon settled across the platform and blocked the way onward.

### The Dragon's Deal

The dragon demanded respect and a promise that nobody would reveal its presence. [Varis](/characters/varis) knelt, and a ring of flame left a magical mark on his thigh. [Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) stabilized [Fenris](/characters/fenris), bringing him back to consciousness.

All three swore silence. In return for the chest of old copper coins, they agreed to travel to [Morgan's Keep](/places/morgans-keep), light a particular rune with coal, and investigate a hidden place there. [Fenris](/characters/fenris) apologized for his earlier approach. The dragon explained that the statues' weapons were bound to old magic, then allowed them to take the treasure.

The chest weighed somewhere between seventy and a hundred kilograms. Carrying it would require two people, and the river gap still lay between them and the exit.

### Fighting Back Through the Tunnels

The party distributed some ancient coins among their pockets and bags before moving out. Goblins attacked as they carried the chest uphill. [Fenris](/characters/fenris) set it down and added fire to [Varis](/characters/varis)'s arrows, while [Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) met the attackers with her axe.

More goblins followed. [Fenris](/characters/fenris)'s attempt to frighten one with the flute failed, so he protected [Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) with *Barkskin* and rooted the surrounding enemies with *Entangle*. [Varis](/characters/varis) kept firing from a distance, and [Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) cut down the trapped attackers. Afterward, they recovered arrows and took ten goblin ears, hoping the trophies might have value.

At the ravine, they secured a new rope crossing and hauled the chest across. [Varis](/characters/varis)'s foot slipped into the hot water and was burned. [Fenris](/characters/fenris) reinforced the bindings with intestines cut from a dead goblin, and the party worked together until everyone and the chest reached the other side. At the exit, the word “Helligjøp” opened the door.

### Two Payments

[Carlos](/characters/carlos) inspected the coins, rusted knives, and chest. The goblin ears were worthless to him, but he offered 583 gold and took the copper chest. The party divided the proceeds, settled debts, and cleaned themselves up before resting.

The following morning, they delivered the unopened box from [Brad](/characters/brad-academy-student) to [Toltec](/characters/toltec) at [the Academy](/places/the-academy). The elderly green dragonborn paid two hundred gold, completing the [courier contract](/events/toltec-box-courier). They had respected the instruction not to inspect its contents.

[Toltec](/characters/toltec) explained some of the differences between lenses for near and distant vision. When [Fenris](/characters/fenris) showed his fey pollen, he learned that it could be used to influence people and that [Lynn Salt](/characters/lynn-salt) could discuss its alchemical uses further. After dividing the payment, they turned back to their equipment and the repaired bow.

### Wedding Plans

That evening, the party discussed attacking [Helga](/characters/helga) at the upcoming wedding. They considered shooting from the gallery, locking the doors, and using fire arrows and binding roots while flute music covered the opening of the attack. Single-use magical arrows cost a hundred gold each, so they also discussed enchanting ordinary ammunition.

These remained plans. The party had not attacked the temple when the session ended.
