## Recap

### Mirbog and the crystal

Within the crystal chamber, [Elowen](/characters/elowen) met [Mirbog](/characters/mirbog), a half-orc guardian sent by Titania to keep a portal closed. The warden had been trapped there as a sacrifice and described the long isolation. Their encounter concerned the shield, sword, and mask that sustained the seal. [Elowen](/characters/elowen) demonstrated her magic, and they performed a ritual that opened a way out.

They emerged on a hill overlooking a fort, where the party reunited. The green crystal now resembled a spear and seemed to possess healing power. Attempts to understand it injured one experimenter, but using it on the dead half-orc who had emerged with [Elowen](/characters/elowen) restored the companion to life in pain and confusion. The party thought the crystal had been halved and might be broken.

The party realized that removing the three relics might release a horde of dead. [Mirbog](/characters/mirbog) gave them a formula to close the portal again, accepted provisions, and asked the way to the nearest town before departing. [Elowen](/characters/elowen) described a chamber with several portals. Although the party considered returning some of the items, they continued toward [Fort John](/places/fort-john).

### The baron's offer

A military camp surrounded [Fort John](/places/fort-john), with smoke rising from the fortress and banners belonging to the general under whom [Varis](/characters/varis) had served. The party met [Lieutenant Markus](/characters/markus-lieutenant) and [Baron Peridott](/characters/baron-peridott). They learned that the arrest of [Karl Marsensen](/characters/karl-marsensen), a local defender of the people's right to food, had prompted the townspeople to storm and occupy the fort.

[Baron Peridott](/characters/baron-peridott) offered to employ them as "independent agents": five gold each if they extracted [Karl](/characters/karl-marsensen), or fifteen each if they resolved the whole situation. One of the baron's family bows was also raised as a possible reward. The party considered negotiating with [Karl](/characters/karl-marsensen)'s wife, [Frida](/characters/frida-marsensen), who led the rebels, or taking food from the baron's stores to win their trust.

### The well and the spider

Scouting revealed rusted gate hinges, a locked side entrance, and a river leading into the fort. [Elowen](/characters/elowen) sent her fish familiar through the waterway to a well inside. It heard commotion above, and she used its position to direct *Stinking Cloud* into the courtyard. People retched and backed away, fearing the well was poisoned or haunted.

With a key to the side door secured, the party settled on a diversion. [Fenris](/characters/fenris) became a giant spider and approached the wall opposite the military camp. The rebels shot at him, drawing their attention away from the entrance.

During the attempt, [Sommerhimmel](/characters/sommerhimmel) appeared through a portal and confronted [Elowen](/characters/elowen) for taking all three relics. The angry messenger pulled her away, and the others lost contact with her.

### Inside the fortress

The remaining infiltrators unlocked the side door. [Varis](/characters/varis) struck a distracted guard with his rapier and knocked him unconscious. He and [Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) entered the courtyard while the rebels rushed to confront the spider. To make themselves appear to be defenders, [Varis](/characters/varis) staged a shot at [Fenris](/characters/fenris), who retreated as though wounded.

The bluff worked. [Frida](/characters/frida-marsensen) praised the newcomers and invited them to speak. She believed the rebels could last a week on the baron's beer despite the unusable well. They still needed to free [Karl](/characters/karl-marsensen), whom they believed was held in the tower or somewhere outside the fortress.

The party considered destroying the beer to weaken the rebels' position. [Fenris](/characters/fenris) changed into a squirrel and prepared to climb the tower. The session ended with him ready to investigate, [Varis](/characters/varis) and [Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) accepted inside the courtyard, and [Elowen](/characters/elowen) missing.
