## Recap

[Varis](/characters/varis) and [Peren](/characters/peren) traveled from [Dior](/places/dior) toward [Morgan's Keep](/places/morgans-keep) while [Fenris](/characters/fenris) and [Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) dealt with the [Burning of Sire](/events/burning-of-sire).

After their confrontation with [Helga](/characters/helga), they chose to stay outside the city. They set aside an immediate prison break and discussed a mining prison in the hills west of [Morgan's Keep](/places/morgans-keep), controlled by a group known as the Twelve. They knew its general location, but needed [Peren](/characters/peren)'s contacts in the capital to find a way forward.

### The Road

They stayed at an inn called [The Thirsty Dwarf](/places/the-thirsty-dwarf) outside the city, then continued along the river through flat, dusty terrain. [Varis](/characters/varis) was weighed down by weapons while [Peren](/characters/peren) moved lighter. They passed farms, fisher folk, and growing crowds of travelers all heading toward [Morgan's Keep](/places/morgans-keep).

At a roadside inn, all rooms were full — they slept in the stable. The meal was memorable: sausages, root vegetables, good bread, and a distinctive local cheese that became a running theme.

### Meeting Lynn Salt

A familiar face entered the inn: [Lynn Salt](/characters/lynn-salt), a dwarven woman with curly hair, a backpack, and a book. They had met her before. She was also heading to [Morgan's Keep](/places/morgans-keep), transporting magical ingredients for [the Academy](/factions/the-academy). She was businesslike and reserved, not offering much detail about her work.

She gave a lesson on local cheese-making — how it was made from a cow named Klara.

While the conversation continued, [Peren](/characters/peren) inspected a locked chest near the counter. Faint runes marked it, and through a gap he glimpsed a box resembling one the party had carried before. He considered opening it, then left it alone rather than risk a magical alarm.

### Meeting Thor

An old man named [Thor](/characters/thor-veteran) sat at their table with his own cheese. [Peren](/characters/peren) asked for a piece. [Thor](/characters/thor-veteran) was heading to [Morgan's Keep](/places/morgans-keep) for work and shared stories: drought had hit the brewing industry, and there were rumors of ghosts and strange happenings around Fortrejon, with a name "Kopp" whispered in connection.

### Card Stories

The party drew storytelling cards. [Peren](/characters/peren) told a formative memory: as a child in the forest, he witnessed mercenaries commit terrible violence — a sight that shattered him but also lit a desire to protect others. He admitted to being a criminal who tries to do good. An old voice at the table spoke about *"an eye for an eye"* and the tension between justice and forgiveness.

### The Potion Shop

[Varis](/characters/varis) and [Peren](/characters/peren) visited a traveling herbalist — [Thorn](/characters/thorn-alchemist) from their [Session 9](/sessions/session-09) sea-serpent outing, operating under a merchant guise. They discussed magical glasses and tried a pair that sharpened nearby objects but did little for the view outside.

[Peren](/characters/peren) brought out fey pollen and a dried surbla leaf. [Thorn](/characters/thorn-alchemist) explained that pollen could produce a healing potion, though its unstable magic might also discolor the drinker or leave a strange smell. While they prepared the ingredients, [Peren](/characters/peren) impulsively threw a reagent into the fire. The flash temporarily blinded him and ruined the surbla mixture.

They commissioned a fairy healing potion from the surviving ingredients. [Varis](/characters/varis) struggled to follow the process, but [Peren](/characters/peren) learned enough to make a basic, low-quality healing mixture himself.

### Ambush in the Night

[Thorn](/characters/thorn-alchemist) poisoned their drinks with a sleep compound, then struck in the stable. His grudge went back to the serpent quest: he had slicked the oars so the party would faint on the island and [Peren](/characters/peren) would fight the snake alone; [Elowen](/characters/elowen) had destroyed his front door in a rage over that trick ([Thorn's Serpent and Fey Visions](/events/thorn-serpent-and-visions)).

[Thorn](/characters/thorn-alchemist) attacked alone. [Peren](/characters/peren) woke groggy and took a knife in the back; he wrestled free and pinned his attacker with a knife to the throat while [Varis](/characters/varis) joined the fight. The brawl left [Thorn](/characters/thorn-alchemist) badly hurt and panicked the horses. When [Peren](/characters/peren) tried to calm one, dizziness overcame him and he collapsed against a stall door. [Varis](/characters/varis) checked his breathing and laid him on his side until he began to recover.

[Varis](/characters/varis) gave [Thorn](/characters/thorn-alchemist) a healing potion to stabilize him. Under questioning, the alchemist admitted his earlier use of a sleeping mixture while trying to justify the attack as retaliation for the damage to his home. [Peren](/characters/peren) took his poisoning notebook and some daggers.

### Departure

A worried woman (the innkeeper's wife) arrived. The party showed their credentials and were asked to leave. They joined [Lynn Salt](/characters/lynn-salt)'s wagon for the remaining journey to [Morgan's Keep](/places/morgans-keep), acquiring two horses from the stable in the process.

The session ended with the party traveling the final stretch toward the capital.
