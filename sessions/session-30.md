## Key Events

- [Helga](/characters/helga) evacuated [King Morgan XIII](/places/kingdom-of-morgan) as guards and council attendants began fighting one another.
- [Varis](/characters/varis)'s magical arrows and [Fenris](/characters/fenris)'s bear claws overcame [Viktor Carnelian](/characters/viktor-carnelian)'s protection. Viktor fell, but his armor glowed and he rose to leave the chamber.
- The cardinal of the [Church of the Triad](/factions/temple-of-tyr) was carried out while a blind priestess continued healing her; her survival remained unknown.
- [General Kamp](/factions/third-army) told [Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) to run. He withdrew with [Gunther](/characters/gunther) and [Molly Alabaster](/characters/molly-alabaster), who stole an amulet hanging on the wall in the king's room.
- [Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) severed [Lady Alabaster](/characters/lady-alabaster)'s lower leg and took her necklace, leaving her alive and bleeding on the floor.
- [Konstantin Carnelian](/characters/konstantin-carnelian) honored his promise to stop pursuing the party. He left by the same route as his brother [Viktor](/characters/viktor-carnelian), while [Old Garreth](/factions/toll-guild) also escaped the chamber.
- [Peren](/characters/peren) recovered memories of his own part in a massacre, then followed [Viktor](/characters/viktor-carnelian) into the corridor as the others withdrew.

## Still Unresolved

- Will the cardinal of the [Church of the Triad](/factions/temple-of-tyr) and [Lady Alabaster](/characters/lady-alabaster) survive their wounds?
- What will happen when [Peren](/characters/peren) catches up with [Viktor Carnelian](/characters/viktor-carnelian), and what did his recovered memories reveal about the massacre?
- Who among the [King's Guard](/factions/kings-guard) served which interests, and what will follow [Old Garreth](/factions/toll-guild)'s departure after the framing confession?

## Recap

The cardinal lay bleeding between the tables after [Viktor Carnelian](/characters/viktor-carnelian)'s attack. Her axe-bearing dwarf rushed him, but his shimmering armor turned aside the blows. Viktor struck the dwarf in the face with his spear shaft and cut across his bare chest. Behind them, the cardinal's blind priestess knelt to pray over the wound in her throat.

### The council divided

[Helga](/characters/helga) pulled [King Morgan XIII](/places/kingdom-of-morgan) from his chair and led him toward the door behind the throne. A guard moved between him and the fighting. [General Kamp](/factions/third-army) withdrew toward the wall with the mage [Molly Alabaster](/characters/molly-alabaster) and the heavily armored dwarf [Gunther](/characters/gunther). Molly gave [Fenris](/characters/fenris) a small smile and a shrug after trying to stop his magic. The general's attendants did not come to the cardinal's aid.

[Old Garreth](/factions/toll-guild), the kingdom's mintmaster, ordered [Konstantin Carnelian](/characters/konstantin-carnelian) toward the party. Konstantin invoked his earlier promise to them and held back. [Peren](/characters/peren) climbed clear of the table where he had landed and poisoned his knives. [Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) rose among the scattered dishes, collected a pair of ornate shoes from beneath a table, and headed for [Viktor](/characters/viktor-carnelian).

A hammer-bearing guard entering the room recognized [Peren](/characters/peren). He was one of [Helga](/characters/helga)'s men, whom the party had attacked, bound, and left behind in [Dior](/places/dior).

[Fenris](/characters/fenris) raised roots and vines around [Old Garreth](/factions/toll-guild)'s group, trapping [Konstantin](/characters/konstantin-carnelian) and several guards. He became a large bear and sent his ice and magma elementals away from [Lady Alabaster](/characters/lady-alabaster) to attack. Konstantin pulled Garreth behind him and shielded him from the flames with his cloak. [Gunther](/characters/gunther) raised his shield in front of [General Kamp](/factions/third-army), while frost settled in [Molly](/characters/molly-alabaster)'s hair.

[Varis](/characters/varis) shot [Viktor](/characters/viktor-carnelian) through a gap in his armor. A guard cut [Lady Alabaster](/characters/lady-alabaster)'s bonds, and she crawled beneath a table with the cardinal's blood on her face.

### Breaking Viktor's protection

[Viktor](/characters/viktor-carnelian) threw his spear into [Varis](/characters/varis)'s shoulder and knocked him down, then drove the cardinal's dwarf back with another forceful blow. The spear returned to his hand. He took up a position in front of [Old Garreth](/factions/toll-guild) and challenged the party to approach.

An enormous scarred man burst through the door and ran across the chamber. The party recognized him as the guard they had seen at [the crypt in Dior](/places/the-crypt). At the wall, [Gunther](/characters/gunther) smashed the ice elemental, releasing a burst of frost. Garreth's guards surrounded and destroyed the magma elemental in an eruption of fire and smoke.

[Peren](/characters/peren) darted at [Viktor](/characters/viktor-carnelian)'s neck. One knife caught on his armor as the returning spear interfered, but the other drew blood. The poison failed to stop him. [Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) swung [Joe](/characters/practical-joe)'s axe, only for the force of Viktor's shield to knock it from her hands. She drew a glass axe and struck again. Her second blow pierced his protection and hit his chest, shattering the axe. His armor glowed, and the bleeding slowed.

[Fenris](/characters/fenris) followed in bear form, biting the shield and clawing at [Viktor](/characters/viktor-carnelian). His magical claws cut through the shimmering protection. Nearby, the guard from [Dior](/places/dior) struck [Peren](/characters/peren) with his hammer. Another guard drove an axe into the attacker's back. Peren recognized his rescuer as the man who had spoken with him before the hearing; the two guards turned on one another.

Back on his feet, [Varis](/characters/varis) saw that ordinary arrows did less harm than the glass axe and bear claws. He chose a magical arrow. When [Viktor](/characters/viktor-carnelian) raised his shield against [Fenris](/characters/fenris), Varis shot beneath his arm. Lightning ran through him, and he dropped the shield.

### The cardinal carried out

[Varis](/characters/varis) called for help for the cardinal. The light from the blind priestess's hands had weakened, and she was growing desperate. The scarred giant reached them, lifted the cardinal, and turned toward the exit. The priestess climbed onto his back and continued healing as he carried them both. The axe-bearing dwarf covered their retreat.

[General Kamp](/factions/third-army) met [Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok)'s eyes and gave her a short warning: "Run." He and [Gunther](/characters/gunther) headed out. [Molly Alabaster](/characters/molly-alabaster) took an amulet hanging on the wall in the king's room, put it on, and left. It was a separate amulet from the [Amulet of the Living](/characters/varis#notable-equipment) carried by [Varis](/characters/varis).

[Viktor](/characters/viktor-carnelian) retreated behind the still-entangled [Konstantin](/characters/konstantin-carnelian). His armor glowed again, and arrows fell from his healing wounds. One of [Old Garreth](/factions/toll-guild)'s men tried to recover the fallen shield, but the cardinal's dwarf cut him down and then followed the cardinal out.

### Flash bombs and old memories

[Peren](/characters/peren) threw a pouch among [Viktor](/characters/viktor-carnelian), [Konstantin](/characters/konstantin-carnelian), and the guards. It burst with a sharp report and a blinding flash, leaving the men stunned while he ran along a table. [Varis](/characters/varis) kept shooting. An ordinary arrow failed to bring Viktor down, but an ice arrow struck and left him collapsed behind the others.

[Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) recovered her axe and called out [Kamp](/factions/third-army)'s warning. [Varis](/characters/varis) began withdrawing with her. [Fenris](/characters/fenris) stayed against the guards blocking the way to [Viktor](/characters/viktor-carnelian), seizing one by the helmet and shaking him before knocking him back.

[Old Garreth](/factions/toll-guild) moved along the wall toward the door. [Peren](/characters/peren) threw another flash bomb directly at him. Its light struck him, but he continued without reacting. Then memories of the guard who had rescued Peren began returning: the man had been present at a massacre in a town. When the guard joined him on the table and greeted him cheerfully, Peren remembered his own part in it. He stood sweating and unable to act as the guilt returned.

### Alabaster beneath the table

[Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) reached [Lady Alabaster](/characters/lady-alabaster)'s hiding place and overturned the table. The guard helping Alabaster fell against another table. Alabaster begged for mercy. Gal'Rok struck her at the knee, severing her lower leg and stopping her escape.

Another guard drove a two-handed sword through the fallen man. [Varis](/characters/varis) watched one royal guard kill another who was already down; their uniforms no longer revealed their allegiances. [Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) tore a necklace from [Lady Alabaster](/characters/lady-alabaster) and left her bleeding. She called to a helmeted special guard who had watched the fighting, but he only followed her movement with his head.

[Varis](/characters/varis) shot one of the men pressing [Fenris](/characters/fenris). Still caught in his memories, [Peren](/characters/peren) took several blows from a swordsman beside the table. His rescuer returned to the fight with the hammer-bearing guard from [Dior](/places/dior). The hammerman was eventually overwhelmed and killed by the other guards.

### Leaving the chamber

[Viktor](/characters/viktor-carnelian)'s armor began glowing again. He rose to his knees and then his feet, limping toward the door before his steps grew stronger. [Konstantin](/characters/konstantin-carnelian) finally broke free of the roots. He told [Fenris](/characters/fenris) plainly that the party could go and that he had no quarrel with them. He withdrew toward the same exit as his brother while [Old Garreth](/factions/toll-guild) left and guards covered their retreat.

[Fenris](/characters/fenris) attacked once more, but four soldiers surrounded him. Their blows broke his bear form and continued falling after he returned to his own body. He released a violent gust of wind, driving two back. In the opening, he became a bear again and withdrew. The battered guards followed briefly before letting him go.

[Varis](/characters/varis) and [Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) had already pulled back as more soldiers entered through the main doors. The cardinal had been carried away, her fate unknown. [Lady Alabaster](/characters/lady-alabaster) remained alive and bleeding on the floor. [Peren](/characters/peren) recovered in time to see [Viktor](/characters/viktor-carnelian) leave, slipped past the swordsman, and followed him into the corridor. [Konstantin](/characters/konstantin-carnelian) took the same route while [Fenris](/characters/fenris) left the chamber.
