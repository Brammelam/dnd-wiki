## Recap

### The Academy Appointment

The full party arrived in [Dior](/places/dior) after four or five days on the road. Beyond the mountain tunnel and its statues, the city surrounded an artificial lake. Houses climbed the morning and evening slopes, but building rules kept them below the surrounding mountain tops. The party took rooms at [Mountain Dew's Rest](/places/mountain-dews-rest) and learned that [the Academy](/places/the-academy) stood on the morning side.

The next morning, [Fenris](/characters/fenris) offered his glasses from [Alverike](/places/alverike) to [Lynn Salt](/characters/lynn-salt), the dwarven receptionist. They did not correct her vision, but she remained interested. After she offered two hundred gold, [Varis](/characters/varis) recognized the attempt to lower the price, and the sale settled at three hundred.

[Lynn Salt](/characters/lynn-salt) arranged an appointment with [Toltec](/characters/toltec) for seven the following evening. The party insisted on delivering [Brad](/characters/brad-academy-student)'s box to him personally. Wary of the receptionist and of wanted notices bearing their descriptions, they considered using false names for guild registration and asked around to establish who their recipient was. An older woman described [Toltec](/characters/toltec) as an elderly green dragonborn in magical clothing, helping them distinguish him from the city's other dragonborn.

### Armor, a Bow, and a Contact

At [Bob Swords](/characters/bob-swords)'s shop, the party considered a cold-enchanted leather vest that could also harm its wearer, a simpler protective vest, and expensive armor whose brief protection came with a severe aftereffect. [Fenris](/characters/fenris) learned that the armor could adapt to his animal forms. They postponed buying any of it.

[Bob Swords](/characters/bob-swords) referred them to [Tore Idle](/characters/tore-idle), who quoted fifty gold to repair [Varis](/characters/varis)'s damaged bow. The work could not restore it exactly as before. Of the alternatives, [Varis](/characters/varis) chose greater power and range at the cost of more difficult aiming, and left the bow for repair.

The merchant also pointed them toward [Carlos](/characters/carlos), who found unofficial work for people without identity papers on the shadow side of the city.

### Carlos's Deal

[Peren](/characters/peren) greeted [Carlos](/characters/carlos) in his own language at a bar. The treasure hunter described a concealed entrance into [the Crypt](/places/the-crypt), a dwarven burial site said to have been sealed for seven hundred years. The entrance lay near a cellar used for illicit wine storage.

[Carlos](/characters/carlos) wanted a quarter of the recovered treasure, with an additional commission if he helped sell it. The party agreed to meet him at ten that evening. He led them past wedding preparations and the guarded main crypt door near the lakeside temple, then into a cellar where loose boards concealed a natural crack in the rock. His bad knee kept him outside, and he warned them about dwarven traps.

### The First Chambers

Inside [the Crypt](/places/the-crypt), the party passed dusty statues, old dwarven inscriptions, and skeletons near a warning to turn back from sacred ground. A rockfall blocked the main route. They chose a smaller door on the left and climbed toward a damaged gallery, avoiding a suspicious hinged step. A hole above the rubble might offer another route, but the climb looked dangerous, so they marked it on their map and returned below.

A narrower passage brought them to a handleless stone door guarded by four dwarf statues carrying a hammer, axe, sword, and morningstar. Each statue had four buttons. After unsuccessful attempts, the party pressed the first button on the first statue, the second on the next, and so on. The door opened.

When [Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) stepped forward, glowing runes sent threads of light around her and pulled her in opposing directions. The others cut her free, but her shoulder was dislocated. Healing eased the injury, and the party prepared to continue deeper into the crypt.
