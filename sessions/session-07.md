## Recap

### Work at the Pits

[Salander](/characters/salander) received [Varis](/characters/varis), [Flint](/characters/flint), and [Peren](/characters/peren) at the fight pits. He said their arrest over the [Crown Prince's murder](/events/crown-prince-murder) appeared to be a setup involving [Helga](/characters/helga) of the [King's Guard](/factions/kings-guard), though he doubted she had acted alone. He repeated the lead to her daughter's wedding in [Dior](/places/dior) and named [Bob Swords](/characters/bob-swords) as a possible ally there. He could find no noble called "Countess Barros," casting doubt on the name given for the party's pursuer.

[Salander](/characters/salander) offered further information in exchange for killing a city guard who obstructed his interests. [Varis](/characters/varis) objected, and the party refused. Instead, they accepted work from [Griselda](/characters/griselda-mud-street), a dwarf with a broken arm who wanted overdue rent collected from tenants on Mud Street. She promised payment and a poison contact for [Peren](/characters/peren).

### Mud Street

[Varis](/characters/varis) kicked in the door of a building where armed hobbits were protecting the tenants. A fight began in the cramped room, and townspeople with clubs joined the defenders. [Peren](/characters/peren)'s poisoned dart failed to penetrate a hobbit's ringmail, but his blade struck home. He claimed the wounded defender would die without an antidote and demanded surrender.

[Varis](/characters/varis) fired at the defenders while [Flint](/characters/flint) fought with his warhammer. One of [Flint](/characters/flint)'s blows killed a hobbit. A surviving companion tried unsuccessfully to save him through prayer. [Griselda](/characters/griselda-mud-street) warned the party against further killing, and the remaining defenders surrendered and paid the rent.

The sight of the wounded and grieving families unsettled [Peren](/characters/peren). [Flint](/characters/flint) argued that the defenders had attacked with weapons first. After bargaining, the party received 15 gold each. [Griselda](/characters/griselda-mud-street) also told them about a retired poison expert living beside the sea on the road to [Dior](/places/dior).

### Konstantin's challenge

Before they could leave [Rinden](/places/rinden), [Konstantin Carnelian](/characters/konstantin-carnelian), a noble bounty hunter carrying a two-handed sword, confronted them over a murder accusation. He demanded a formal duel. The party mocked the challenge and attacked, with [Peren](/characters/peren) opening with a poisoned dart.

[Konstantin](/characters/konstantin-carnelian) withstood their attacks and cut down [Flint](/characters/flint), leaving him unconscious and bleeding. [Peren](/characters/peren) and [Varis](/characters/varis) continued fighting. An arrow drove the bounty hunter to one knee, but he recovered and struck down [Peren](/characters/peren) as well.

With [Varis](/characters/varis) left standing alone, enforcers from [the Crabs](/factions/the-crabs)' territory intervened. Their leader, known as the Claw, ordered [Konstantin](/characters/konstantin-carnelian) away. He withdrew with a promise of vengeance. The enforcers carried the party back to the fight pits, where [Flint](/characters/flint) and [Peren](/characters/peren) were stabilized. During their recovery, [Varis](/characters/varis) obtained a map for the journey toward [Dior](/places/dior).
