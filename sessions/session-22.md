## Recap

[Fenris](/characters/fenris) and [Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) rode toward the smoke while [Varis](/characters/varis) and [Peren](/characters/peren) headed to [Morgan's Keep](/places/morgans-keep).

### The Journey

They rode along the river through evening and night, passing small farms. The smell of burning grew stronger. After hours, they camped at a clearing by the riverbank. The next morning, riders in fine but sweaty clothes passed — tired and worried refugees. They learned the forest across the river was burning and houses near the treeline had been consumed. No word had come from [Kalén](/places/kalen).

As the day wore on, more refugees streamed past: mothers with children, elderly on donkeys, men carrying what little they could save. They heard that soldiers had helped most residents escape, but the fire had spread across the river and destroyed many houses. They continued toward the town to help anyone still trapped.

### The Rescue

[Anne Knutsen](/characters/anne-knutsen) came running, covered in ash, screaming that her father was trapped and would burn. They rode through pockets of fire — [Fenris](/characters/fenris) calmed the reluctant horse — and found [Knut Pilsen](/characters/knut-pilsen) pinned under fallen burning timber near the river, his hair on fire.

[Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) threw her cloak over the flames and stamped them out despite her fear of fire. [Fenris](/characters/fenris) carried a floating mass of water from the river and doused the timber before transforming into a bear. Together they lifted the obstruction and carried [Knut](/characters/knut-pilsen) into the shallows, where healing magic steadied his breathing. [Anne](/characters/anne-knutsen) had climbed onto their horse during the rescue.

[Knut](/characters/knut-pilsen) called for Betty, his horse, who had fled across the river. [Fenris](/characters/fenris) followed the tracks while [Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) stayed with father and daughter. They agreed to meet again once everyone was safe.

### Sire

Approaching the town, [Fenris](/characters/fenris) followed Betty's hoof prints and found frightened horses among burning textiles. He calmed them and sent them back across the river to wait in safety.

[Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) plunged into the burning town looking for salvage. In a smith's workshop, she found two rapiers under debris. She wrapped the hot metal in cloth and escaped as the building collapsed behind her.

### The Pyromancer Fight

Emerging from the smithy, [Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) was hit by a fireball from the town square. A scarred, burned man stood there holding a book, smoke swirling around him in unnatural patterns.

[Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) charged and severed the hand holding the book. He retaliated with a close blast of fire, throwing her against a wall.

Hearing the explosion, [Fenris](/characters/fenris) used river water to beat back the flames on a bridge and crossed it just before it collapsed. He transformed into a giant constricting snake and approached the pyromancer. [Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) recovered and struck again, shattering her glass axe in a blow that left the man badly wounded.

The pyromancer drank from a bottle and raised an invisible barrier that kept [Fenris](/characters/fenris) from constricting him. He unleashed another explosion at close range. [Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) was thrown through the air and knocked unconscious; [Fenris](/characters/fenris) was left burned and almost without strength. The pyromancer died in his own blast, his legs reduced to charred stumps.

### Aftermath

[Fenris](/characters/fenris) crawled forward and found a glowing medallion among the remains. It was too hot to hold, so he wrapped it in cloth and collected burned book pages and the pyromancer's remains. [Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) woke in panic and ran from the flames before he could speak to her. He cast a healing spell over her, then searched the street and found a purse marked "Sigurd" on another body.

With the medallion and evidence secured, they headed back toward the horses.

## Loot
