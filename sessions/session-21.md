## Recap

### Helga's Account

At [Tyr Moir](/places/tyr-moir), [Varis](/characters/varis), [Fenris](/characters/fenris), and [Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) confronted [Helga](/characters/helga) about the [Crown Prince murder](/events/crown-prince-murder). She acknowledged arresting them and remained suspicious. They argued that they had been framed and that innocent elves had already confessed under torture and been executed.

[Helga](/characters/helga) pointed them toward the lieutenant who filed the arrest report, [Preben Jet](/characters/lieutenant-jet). She suspected pressure connected to his wife's debts and the [Humanus Association](/factions/humanus-association), whose headquarters were in [Morgan's Keep](/places/morgans-keep). His wife was imprisoned, giving the party another person to seek out.

[Helga](/characters/helga) offered to use her network to reduce attention on the fugitives if they kept a low profile. She would not act openly against powerful people or acknowledge them as her clients. If they approached her again, she warned, her duty to the [King's Guard](/factions/kings-guard) would require their arrest. She also pointed to the cardinal's truth-compelling power at the King's Council as a possible route to proving their account.

### Threats and Departure

[Fenris](/characters/fenris) thought [Helga](/characters/helga) was withholding something. [Varis](/characters/varis) raised his bow, and [Fenris](/characters/fenris) put a hand on it to calm the exchange. The captive guard was brought forward, and a drawn knife accompanied a final warning, but [Helga](/characters/helga) refused to say more.

The party cut the guard's bonds, warned [Helga](/characters/helga) of retaliation against her family if they were betrayed, and withdrew to plan their next move.

### A Possible Prison Break

At the inn, they considered taking [Lieutenant Jet](/characters/lieutenant-jet)'s wife out of prison as a source of information or a hostage. They discussed disguises, allies in elven settlements, and contacts who could help them return to [Morgan's Keep](/places/morgans-keep).

[Bob Swords](/characters/bob-swords) bought back the wedding invitations for a fraction of their price and took the rented clothes. He discussed the [Humanus Association](/factions/humanus-association)'s reputation with them. Its likely restriction to human members would make infiltration difficult.

[Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) suggested using [Fenris](/characters/fenris)'s ethereal blade to move a prisoner beyond a cell wall. They discussed striking through a window and arranging the space to influence where wielder and target reappeared. The idea remained a proposal; no prison break took place in this session.

### The Military Camp

The party left [Dior](/places/dior) in small groups through the mountain tunnel. Outside, a large military camp stood along the river, with soldiers and horses preparing to move. Smoke drifted from the west.

[Varis](/characters/varis) approached a young soldier and showed his City Watch insignia. The man described service with the fourth company and explained that church service provided food and shelter for him and his family. Talk around the camp suggested a march toward [Morgan's Keep](/places/morgans-keep), though the timing and orders remained unclear. Rumors linked the mobilization to the cardinal's departure from the capital after the arrests there.

The party worried that a larger conflict was approaching. They also wondered whether the smoke came from the forest where they had earlier recovered artifacts, but had no confirmation of its cause.

### Smoke Across the River

[Fenris](/characters/fenris) used *Speak with Plants* on an old pine by the river. It answered in simple impressions: “rush. dry. safe.” The river still flowed, the land was unusually dry, and the tree had noticed smoke. Its roots did not reach across the water, so it could not tell him what was happening farther west.

The fire appeared extensive and perhaps two days away. [Varis](/characters/varis) remembered [Thor](/characters/thor-veteran), an old military contact in [Morgan's Keep](/places/morgans-keep). The party weighed returning to the capital against investigating the smoke and discussed splitting up, with only one horse available. They intended to regroup in the capital after pursuing the separate leads.

## Still Unresolved

- Could [Lieutenant Jet](/characters/lieutenant-jet) or his imprisoned wife connect the false report to the [Humanus Association](/factions/humanus-association)?
- What had caused the fire, and when would the soldiers march?
