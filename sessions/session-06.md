## Recap

### Passage to the island

At [Rinden](/places/rinden)'s Old Town docks, the party watched a donkey-powered barge carry goods and invited guests to [Scully Stronghold](/places/scully-stronghold). Passage required an invitation from [Lady Amber](/characters/lady-amber). [Elowen](/characters/elowen) recognized [Brad](/characters/brad-academy-student) among the waiting passengers and claimed she was searching for a lost love on the island. He agreed to take her as his guest.

[Elowen](/characters/elowen) cast *Invisibility* on [Varis](/characters/varis), who slipped aboard with the cargo. [Flint](/characters/flint) and [Fenris](/characters/fenris) remained at the mainland docks while the first pair crossed. After landing, [Brad](/characters/brad-academy-student) left for another engagement. [Elowen](/characters/elowen) placed her fish familiar beneath the dock to watch arriving shipments, while [Varis](/characters/varis) scouted the gate and the stronghold's interior.

### Lady Amber's visitors

During the dockside watch, the party saw [Toll Guild](/factions/toll-guild) representatives and enforcers take the trader Hagenfeld into custody. The party did not intervene.

Inside the stronghold, [Elowen](/characters/elowen) spotted [Lady Amber](/characters/lady-amber) meeting privately with a priest of the [Church of the Triad](/factions/temple-of-tyr). [Varis](/characters/varis) moved close enough to overhear. The priest asked her to help the hungry and exploited people of [Rinden](/places/rinden). She expressed sympathy but distrusted the church's motives and its elven leadership, promising only to consider the request.

[Brad](/characters/brad-academy-student) visited next. He said [the Academy](/factions/the-academy) had found clues to [Lady Amber](/characters/lady-amber)'s missing sister and requested 200 gold to continue the search. Despite her doubts, she instructed her treasurer to pay him.

### The cargo and the diversion

Back at the mainland docks, [Fenris](/characters/fenris) became a forest mouse and hid in [Flint](/characters/flint)'s sleeve. [Flint](/characters/flint) pretended to be drunk, then used a staged stumble to toss the mouse onto the barge when the guards refused him passage.

A cat attacked [Fenris](/characters/fenris) as he investigated a barrel, forcing him back into his usual form. He hid among the crates and used his ability to speak with animals to persuade the cat to leave him alone. Beneath the wine, he found a hidden compartment containing dried leaves. He took a sample and hid beneath the vessel as it reached the island.

The diversion left [Flint](/characters/flint) in custody. [Father Terry](/characters/father-terry) accused him of an earlier attack on a woman known as Old Beate, and the guards held him while they checked the story. When she was brought in, her confused complaints about "long-eared folks" did not support the accusation. The guards released [Flint](/characters/flint) with a warning.

### A lead toward Dior

The party reunited, and [Fenris](/characters/fenris) identified the smuggled leaves as sourleaf, a narcotic. They took the sample to [Salander](/characters/salander) at the fight pits. He identified its source as a northern region called Darukesh, then addressed their original question about the [Crown Prince's murder](/events/crown-prince-murder).

[Salander](/characters/salander) said the party's framing had been deliberate and involved someone connected to the palace guard. He directed them to [Captain Helga](/characters/helga), who had gone to [Dior](/places/dior) for her daughter's wedding preparations, and warned that they would need leverage to make her talk. He also offered a contact with a local weapons dealer, [Bob](/characters/bob-swords), and paid each party member five gold. They prepared to follow the lead north.
