## Recap

### Inside the Temple

With two days until the wedding, the party went to the temple on [Dior](/places/dior)'s lake. Workers were arranging chairs and decorations. [Peren](/characters/peren) tried to pass himself off as one of them, but a guard turned him away.

[Fenris](/characters/fenris) became a cat and slipped inside. While exploring the altar and columns, he knocked over a large vase of flowers. The crash drew the guards' attention, allowing [Peren](/characters/peren) to enter and find the stairs below. [Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) climbed up to survey the building while [Varis](/characters/varis) kept watch outside.

The lower rooms included storage, a work and dining area, and a training room with armor and practice weapons. Some rooms extended below the waterline. [Peren](/characters/peren) opened one locked door with an improvised pick. Upstairs, the party found decorated prayer rooms, some requiring payment, and an instrument store. Two doors remained locked.

[Fenris](/characters/fenris) continued his diversion through the curtains and stairs. When a servant chased him with a broom, he jumped onto the man's head and scratched him before escaping into the crowd. The party regrouped with a clearer picture of the temple's levels and routes, though they still needed to identify who held the remaining keys.

### Watching Helga

[Helga](/characters/helga) appeared among the workers with soldiers at her side, directing the preparations. The party withdrew to plan. They discussed their paired compasses: one was damaged and no longer pointed usefully. Repairing it or disguising it as jewelry might make it suitable for tracking, but a visibly broken object could alert their target.

[Fenris](/characters/fenris) followed [Helga](/characters/helga) in cat form as she moved between the temple, a workshop, and a substantial manor. Its walls were three to four meters high, the gaps between rooftops were difficult to cross, and well-equipped guards watched the gate. Further scouting in cat form failed to secure a way inside, even after attempts to gain the guards' favor by catching mice.

[Peren](/characters/peren) later saw [Helga](/characters/helga) speaking with an unidentified woman on a terrace. The party watched for several hours, looking for an opportunity to reach her without the guards.

### Equipment and Other Approaches

At [Bob Swords](/characters/bob-swords)'s shop, the party obtained fire, ice, and lightning arrows and negotiated for a grappling hook. The merchant promised to investigate the temple's plans, wall thickness, drainage, and interior rooms, and asked them to return the following evening.

At Nymph's Tears, a shop run by a bald halfling, they asked about truth potions and other ways to influence someone. The shopkeeper refused to sell openly illegal preparations. They bought a strong laxative and a more expensive, less unpleasant-tasting version, along with four healing potions at a bulk discount. Sleep medicine and ways to isolate [Helga](/characters/helga) during the wedding remained under discussion.

The party also considered eavesdropping, an anonymous threat, or compromising the bridegroom. A relic shop could not provide a device to capture an image and suggested [the Academy](/places/the-academy), but it had closed by the time they arrived. They returned to the inn with those alternatives still untested.

### The Knives

[Peren](/characters/peren) showed the rusted blades taken from [the Crypt](/places/the-crypt). They discussed wounds that continued bleeding and became worse with repeated cuts, as well as whether armor or bandaging could limit the effect. His aversion to blood made the weapons difficult for him to use.

With one day left before the wedding, the party rested. They still needed to settle how they would obtain answers from [Helga](/characters/helga) without bringing the city's attention down on them.
