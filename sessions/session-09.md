## Recap

### Thorn's lessons

[Varis](/characters/varis), [Peren](/characters/peren), and [Elowen](/characters/elowen) crossed difficult coastal ground to find [Thorn](/characters/thorn-alchemist), the retired poison expert recommended by [Griselda](/characters/griselda-mud-street). He lived in a house with a small stone tower between their camp and [Havfika](/places/havfika).

[Thorn](/characters/thorn-alchemist) was reluctant to return to his old work, but agreed to teach [Elowen](/characters/elowen). He described Dobbelsvin, a mixture that disoriented its victim into attacking those nearby, and warned that mistakes in brewing could be deadly. He then sent the party to an offshore island to obtain venom from a sea serpent.

### The serpent island

[Thorn](/characters/thorn-alchemist) had coated the boat's oars with a sleeping potion to leave [Peren](/characters/peren) facing the serpent alone. Several companions collapsed when they reached the island. The serpent emerged from a cave, and attempts to approach it ended in a fight.

The party endured poisonous bites and a cloud of venom before driving the weakened serpent back into its cave. They survived and returned to the mainland. Furious at the oar trick, [Elowen](/characters/elowen) smashed [Thorn](/characters/thorn-alchemist)'s front door. Despite the confrontation, she received further instruction in handling the venom safely and more advanced recipes.

### The fey calling

While the others rested, [Elowen](/characters/elowen) saw visions of fire and two captives called Grimm and Bobble. To help them, she was instructed to prepare a ritual circle with a friend's stick, three gold coins, and a life. She told the party about the calling, and they began gathering what they needed. They caught an animal in a snare and kept it alive while they prepared.

The party's trail continued into the [Fey Realm](/places/fey-realm). After passing a camp of satyrs and gnomes, they followed signs through the forest to a guarded wagon. [Peren](/characters/peren) tried to approach unseen but was spotted, and a fight broke out. [Varis](/characters/varis) joined him while [Elowen](/characters/elowen) supported them with magic and struck one opponent with fire.

After the fight, the survivors described themselves as traders and petty criminals. They had heard of two gnomes recently taken prisoner and moved toward another camp deeper in the forest. The party suspected these were Grimm and Bobble, giving them a direction to continue the search.
