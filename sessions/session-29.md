## Key Events

- [Lady Alabaster](/characters/lady-alabaster) admitted framing the party for the [Crown Prince murder](/events/crown-prince-murder) and named [Old Garreth](/factions/toll-guild) as the person who ordered it.
- [Viktor Carnelian](/characters/viktor-carnelian) tried to silence Alabaster and critically wounded the cardinal.
- The party fought back; the session ended mid-fight in the King's Council chamber at [Morgan's Keep](/places/morgans-keep).

## Still Unresolved

- Can the party protect the cardinal and keep [Lady Alabaster](/characters/lady-alabaster) alive to testify?
- What is [Viktor Carnelian](/characters/viktor-carnelian)'s connection to [Old Garreth](/factions/toll-guild)?

These questions reflect where the session ended; their answers are not yet known.

## Recap

Morning found the party on the quay among wet survivors and the smoke of the ruined ship. Townspeople brought blankets and food while a weary watchman thanked them. [Lady Alabaster](/characters/lady-alabaster) lay bound and gagged nearby. A helmeted city guard said the incident would be investigated and offered to take statements from anyone willing to explain what happened. The crowd began shouting for the King's Council and surged toward the palace.

As people organized aid, a man who knew people in the city's defense pulled [Varis](/characters/varis) aside. He offered him work if he ever needed it and told him where to find them. [Peren](/characters/peren) covered a rescued older man with a blanket and stayed close while help gathered around the survivors.

### The palace gates

[Varis](/characters/varis), [Peren](/characters/peren), [Fenris](/characters/fenris), and [Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) led a growing crowd from the harbor toward the palace. Broad streets and walled gardens replaced the working district. City guards held the crowd back as royal guards closed the gate and turned the party away. When they heard Alabaster's name and the accusation that she had transported elves as slaves, they opened the gate. [Helga](/characters/helga) escorted the party into the courtyard.

Inside the palace they passed portraits, statues, arms, armor, and curiosities collected by [King Morgan XIII](/places/kingdom-of-morgan). [Helga](/characters/helga) seated them with their prisoner and gave them food and time to recover while she summoned the council.

[Peren](/characters/peren) explained its members: [General Kamp](/factions/third-army) of the [Third Army](/factions/third-army), the cardinal of the [Church of the Triad](/factions/temple-of-tyr), [Old Garreth](/factions/toll-guild), and the king. They discussed whether the cardinal's truth ritual could be trusted and how to detect deception within it.

They also discussed rumors that [King Morgan XIII](/places/kingdom-of-morgan) had become more interested in collecting old artifacts than governing, and that the long peace had softened his rule. These remained rumors as they waited for him to arrive.

While they waited, a palace guard recognized [Peren](/characters/peren) and sat opposite him. The two exchanged memories of their years as mercenaries before the guard joined the court. The conversation remained polite but guarded; their recollections did not settle every detail of the past.

### The King's Council

[Konstantin Carnelian](/characters/konstantin-carnelian) entered with a shield-bearing guard and an old man with gold-colored eyes. [Fenris](/characters/fenris) applauded alone; [Varis](/characters/varis) called out, "Nice sword, dork!" [General Kamp](/factions/third-army) of the [Third Army](/factions/third-army) followed with a large man and a woman whose face bore an old burn scar. [King Morgan XIII](/places/kingdom-of-morgan) arrived beside the cardinal, [Helga](/characters/helga), and two dwarves of the [Triad](/factions/temple-of-tyr): a northerner dressed in fur and a blindfolded woman.

[Varis](/characters/varis) began explaining the plot against the party while [Peren](/characters/peren) watched [Lady Alabaster](/characters/lady-alabaster)'s uneasy attempts to defend herself. The cardinal drew a shining circle around her and compelled the truth. Asked whether she had arranged for the party to be accused of murdering the [Crown Prince](/events/crown-prince-murder), she answered yes. Asked who had ordered it, she pointed to [Old Garreth](/factions/toll-guild).

### Violence in the council chamber

[Viktor Carnelian](/characters/viktor-carnelian) lunged forward with a spear to silence Alabaster. The cardinal caught the shaft, but Viktor drove the weapon into the cardinal's chest. [Fenris](/characters/fenris) summoned an ice elemental and a fire elemental between Alabaster and her attackers. [Varis](/characters/varis) shot Viktor in the shoulder with an ice arrow.

[Varis](/characters/varis) fired again at close range, striking the edge of Viktor's arm armor without stopping him. [Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) tried to cut the spear, but Viktor pulled it away before her blow landed. [Fenris](/characters/fenris) healed the cardinal's chest wound enough to give her some strength back. [Peren](/characters/peren) struck Viktor beneath his shield, though his armor blunted the knife's penetration.

Viktor answered with a sweeping strike that hurled [Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) through the chamber and threw [Peren](/characters/peren) into a wall. He drove the spear into the cardinal's throat; it nearly killed her, but did not pass all the way through. Guards rushed through the corridors as the fight paused in the chaos around the wounded cardinal, with Alabaster's confession heard and the council conspiracy exposed but not yet resolved.
