## Recap

### The Tower and Beer Cellar

[Fenris](/characters/fenris) climbed the tower in squirrel form and entered a room occupied by a long-haired man. He continued past card-playing guards and a locked storeroom to the ground floor, where people were moving supplies. While trying to drink from a beer barrel, he fell and was soaked. A man caught the squirrel and showed him to someone called Karl.

Meanwhile, [Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) persuaded someone to take her and [Varis](/characters/varis) into a hidden beer cellar. They drank with Berg, Frank, and Sofia and learned about the guard rotations. Several of the soldiers had been pressed into service by family or circumstance. Hungry and dissatisfied, they hoped [Frida Marsensen](/characters/frida-marsensen) could find a way out of the siege.

### Fenris in the Well

[Fenris](/characters/fenris) bit one of his captors. The man struck him against a wall, ending his animal form. He cast *Entangle* and *Wind Wall*, then held the two men at bay with his ethereal blade and shield. He nearly revealed the plan to rescue [Karl Marsensen](/characters/karl-marsensen), but the captors did not appear to understand the prisoner's importance.

Officers noticed the disturbance and withdrew. When [Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) and [Varis](/characters/varis) approached, [Fenris](/characters/fenris)'s attempts to signal them failed. He shouted that they did not know him, ran out of the tower, and raised an alarm about poisoned water. He jumped into the well and hid below while people covered its opening.

[Frida Marsensen](/characters/frida-marsensen) heard the guards' account and recognized the work of a druid, probably sent by [Baron Peridott](/characters/baron-peridott). She ordered tighter surveillance and extra guards through the night.

### Karl's Proposal

[Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) and [Varis](/characters/varis) reached [Karl Marsensen](/characters/karl-marsensen)'s room. The prisoner wanted to negotiate with [Baron Peridott](/characters/baron-peridott). He warned that immediate surrender would leave the townspeople facing winter famine and offered gold and valuable goods in exchange for freedom and a chance to bargain.

The party weighed that offer against the roughly seventy soldiers outside the fort and the risk that a rescue would trigger an attack. [Karl Marsensen](/characters/karl-marsensen)'s build resembled [Baron Peridott](/characters/baron-peridott)'s, suggesting a possible disguise or staged execution to pressure [Frida Marsensen](/characters/frida-marsensen). Smuggling the prisoner out in a rug was another option.

[Varis](/characters/varis)'s ties to the besieging soldiers complicated the decision. The pair also considered sabotaging the food and water stores to force negotiations without a battle. They had not settled on a plan while [Fenris](/characters/fenris) remained in the well.

## Still Unresolved
