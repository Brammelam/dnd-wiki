## Key Events

- [Thor](/characters/thor-veteran)'s allies offered access to the King's Council if the party captured [Lady Alabaster](/characters/lady-alabaster) themselves
- The party pursued Alabaster to the harbor and boarded her departing vessel
- A shattered purple crystal spread mind-locking smoke; the ship's sail caught fire
- The party discovered and freed imprisoned elves below deck
- [Fenris](/characters/fenris) rescued [Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) and [Peren](/characters/peren) from drowning after an anchor fouled their rope
- [Lady Alabaster](/characters/lady-alabaster) was captured alive for questioning

## Recap

In a decaying apartment overlooking a quiet noble courtyard, [Thor](/characters/thor-veteran) asked how the party meant to catch [Lady Alabaster](/characters/lady-alabaster). His allies could arrange a hearing before the King's Council, but political risk kept them from making the arrest themselves. The party chose to watch the district and take Alabaster while she was moving.

### Preparations

They rested and prepared for the abduction. [Peren](/characters/peren) mixed a bottle of black fog and a pouch of disorienting powder. [Fenris](/characters/fenris) practiced small nature magic and explored the speed and force of his improved Wild Shape. [Varis](/characters/varis) prepared fire, water, and magic that would hide their trail. [Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) kept her strength ready for doors and other obstacles.

At [Knut Pilsen](/characters/knut-pilsen)'s camp they also settled a mundane concern: a merchant had offered [Peren](/characters/peren) two hundred gold for ten kilograms of cheese, to be delivered the next day. They discussed a guard or a weapon for the courier, but money was scarce. [Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) insisted that they explain the tracking compass openly as a way to keep him safe. They agreed to send the courier early the next morning with the compass and clear instructions, taking care that the arrangement also served [Knut](/characters/knut-pilsen) and [Anne](/characters/anne-knutsen).

Then the message arrived: Alabaster was heading for the harbor. Four horses waited.

### Boarding

The party rode through torchlit streets toward the quay, where a small sailing vessel was already casting off. [Peren](/characters/peren) hooked the hull with a rope and swung aboard. [Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) cleared the gap from the crates, [Varis](/characters/varis) climbed after them, and [Fenris](/characters/fenris) entered the water before changing into a giant octopus and fastening tentacles to the ship.

[Lady Alabaster](/characters/lady-alabaster) stood on the upper deck while a broad captain raised a heavy crossbow. [Varis](/characters/varis)'s arrows punched into the captain's armor. [Fenris](/characters/fenris) reached for Alabaster without killing her, but she held a dark-purple crystal that released smoke across the deck. A thrown lantern struck the rigging and set the sail alight. With its rudder also damaged in the fighting, the vessel drifted farther from shore. [Varis](/characters/varis) shattered the crystal with a second arrow; its smoke locked him rigid while [Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) resisted it.

[Peren](/characters/peren) stabbed the captain from the shadows. [Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) charged with her glass axe, which broke on her final blow, and took a crossbow bolt to the face. [Fenris](/characters/fenris) wrapped Alabaster in a tentacle and held her above the burning deck. The crystal's smoke had caught her as well, leaving her trapped by both its magic and his grip.

### Prisoners below

Through an open hatch, [Fenris](/characters/fenris) saw elves chained behind iron bars. [Peren](/characters/peren) killed the captain, then froze beneath a violent memory of another burning ship and a bleeding child. [Fenris](/characters/fenris) returned to his own form and cast water over the sails. The fire died, but the flood crashed through the hold and forced the vessel lower into the river.

[Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) broke gates and doors while [Peren](/characters/peren) recovered and opened the remaining cells. [Varis](/characters/varis), free of the crystal's hold, organized the evacuation. Among the prisoners he recognized a half-orc woman with bright green marks along her neck, whom the party had met while traveling with [Elowen](/characters/elowen). [Fenris](/characters/fenris) pulled [Lady Alabaster](/characters/lady-alabaster) and a trapped armored man from the wreck. [Peren](/characters/peren) searched a cabin long enough to find gold, a signet ring, and a fine dress before squeezing through its window as beams blocked the door.

### The swim ashore

[Peren](/characters/peren) tied a rope to [Lady Alabaster](/characters/lady-alabaster) and kept hold of her as she struggled in the water. [Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) sank, and his attempt to bring her up faltered when their rope caught around a heavy anchor. [Fenris](/characters/fenris), in octopus form, lifted the anchor and dragged the linked swimmers toward shore. The pull scraped them across the riverbed, but brought them within reach of safety.

[Fenris](/characters/fenris) returned when [Peren](/characters/peren) became trapped at a submerged window. He pulled him free, forced water from his lungs, and healed him on the bank. Once he recovered, [Peren](/characters/peren) secured [Lady Alabaster](/characters/lady-alabaster)'s bonds.

At dawn, the rescued elves lay coughing beside the river. The ship smoldered behind them, and [Lady Alabaster](/characters/lady-alabaster) remained alive and bound. The party finally had their prisoner and witnesses for the council.
