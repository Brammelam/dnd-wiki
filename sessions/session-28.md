## Recap

In a decaying apartment overlooking a quiet noble courtyard, [Thor](/characters/thor-veteran) asked how the party meant to catch [Lady Alabaster](/characters/lady-alabaster). His allies could arrange a hearing before the King's Council, but political risk kept them from making the arrest themselves. The party chose to watch the district and take Alabaster while she was moving.

### Preparations

They rested and prepared for the abduction. Peren mixed a bottle of black fog and a pouch of disorienting powder. Fenris practiced small nature magic and explored the speed and force of his improved Wild Shape. Varis prepared fire, water, and magic that would hide their trail. Gal'Rok kept her strength ready for doors and other obstacles.

At [Knut Pilsen](/characters/knut-pilsen)'s camp they also settled a mundane concern: a merchant had offered two hundred gold for ten kilograms of cheese. They planned to send a courier the next morning with clear instructions and a tracking compass, balancing the new trade against the danger on the roads.

Then the message arrived: Alabaster was heading for the harbor. Four horses waited.

### Boarding

The party rode through torchlit streets toward the quay, where a small sailing vessel was already casting off. Peren hooked the hull with a rope and swung aboard. Gal'Rok cleared the gap from the crates, Varis climbed after them, and Fenris entered the water before changing into a giant octopus and fastening tentacles to the ship.

Lady Alabaster stood on the upper deck while a broad captain raised a heavy crossbow. Varis's arrows punched into the captain's armor. Fenris reached for Alabaster without killing her, but she held a dark-purple crystal that released smoke across the deck. A thrown lantern struck the rigging and set the sail alight. Varis shattered the crystal with a second arrow; its smoke locked him rigid while Gal'Rok resisted it.

Peren stabbed the captain from the shadows. Gal'Rok charged with her glass axe, which broke on her final blow, and took a crossbow bolt to the face. Fenris wrapped Alabaster in a tentacle and held her above the burning deck.

### Prisoners below

Through an open hatch, Fenris saw elves chained behind iron bars. Peren killed the captain, then froze beneath a violent memory of another burning ship and a bleeding child. Fenris returned to his own form and cast water over the sails. The fire died, but the flood crashed through the hold and forced the vessel lower into the river.

Gal'Rok broke gates and doors while Peren recovered and opened the remaining cells. Varis organized the evacuation. Fenris pulled Lady Alabaster and a trapped armored man from the wreck. Peren searched a cabin long enough to find gold, a signet ring, and a fine dress before squeezing through its window as beams blocked the door.

The swim ashore nearly killed them. A rope tangled around an anchor and dragged Gal'Rok under. Fenris hauled the anchor and the linked swimmers toward land, then returned when Peren became stuck in a submerged window. He pulled Peren free, forced water from his lungs, and healed him on the bank.

At dawn, the rescued elves lay coughing beside the river. The ship smoldered behind them, and Lady Alabaster remained alive and bound. The party finally had their prisoner and witnesses for the council.

## Key Events

- Thor's allies offered access to the King's Council if the party captured Lady Alabaster themselves
- The party pursued Alabaster to the harbor and boarded her departing vessel
- A shattered purple crystal spread mind-locking smoke; the ship's sail caught fire
- The party discovered and freed imprisoned elves below deck
- Fenris rescued Gal'Rok and Peren from drowning after an anchor fouled their rope
- Lady Alabaster was captured alive for questioning
