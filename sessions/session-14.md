## Recap

### Sabotaging the Supplies

[Frida Marsensen](/characters/frida-marsensen) warned the crowd around the well that [Baron Peridott](/characters/baron-peridott) was using magic and that any animal might be dangerous. Hidden below, [Fenris](/characters/fenris) made the water rise and turn green, later adding an unpleasant smell. The water appeared poisoned; he was using magic to alter its appearance and smell.

[Varis](/characters/varis) slipped into the cellar and punctured the beer barrels with his rapier. His attempt to establish an alibi by slipping in spilled beer ended with him striking a barrel and soaking his clothes. An officer threatened to whip him, but he persuaded the crowd to let him go. He borrowed oversized noble clothes while his own dried, then joined [Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) for a four-hour watch on the northern wall.

The following morning, [Varis](/characters/varis) followed [Frida Marsensen](/characters/frida-marsensen) into the tower under the pretense of cleaning. With a bucket and mop, he reached a door where he overheard her plan to send a messenger to [Dior](/places/dior) and expose [Baron Peridott](/characters/baron-peridott)'s tactics. He and [Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) left on the excuse of gathering supplies and reunited with [Fenris](/characters/fenris) outside.

### A Negotiated Surrender

[Markus](/characters/markus-lieutenant) warned the party that [Konstantin Carnelian](/characters/konstantin-carnelian) was looking for them over the deaths of [Lady Alabaster](/characters/lady-alabaster)'s sons. They waited until the bounty hunter left [Baron Peridott](/characters/baron-peridott)'s tent before reporting the sabotage and the planned appeal to [Dior](/places/dior). The baron promised thirteen gold each if the negotiations succeeded. [Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) reluctantly accepted his intention to punish the town, arguing that the rebel leadership had also acted coercively.

The first meeting with [Frida Marsensen](/characters/frida-marsensen) failed. A rider escaped toward [Dior](/places/dior), beyond the reach of [Markus](/characters/markus-lieutenant)'s men. After thirsty townspeople began deserting the next day, the two sides agreed to exchange limited food rations for surrender of the fort.

Each adventurer received thirteen gold. [Varis](/characters/varis) also chose a finely balanced, nonmagical bow over a damaged bow covered in runes.

### Leaving Fort John

News reached the party that the elven council in [Morgan's Keep](/places/morgans-keep) had been arrested. Rumors placed the cardinal in [Dior](/places/dior), where [Helga](/characters/helga) was also staying. They packed the heavy metal box from [Brad](/characters/brad-academy-student) onto their single horse and prepared to continue the [courier journey](/events/toltec-box-courier).

Before they left, a lieutenant advised [Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) to seek [Thor](/characters/thor-veteran), an old military comrade now working in intelligence, if she needed help in [Morgan's Keep](/places/morgans-keep). He could often be found at an inn called the Empty Barrel.

### The Pond Ambush

On the road toward [Dior](/places/dior), the travelers found a fallen tree and more than ten armed men blocking the way. [Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) approached openly while [Varis](/characters/varis) and [Fenris](/characters/fenris) hid at the forest edge. When the apparent bandits demanded her valuables, she killed a spearman with her glass axe. He and the other men dissolved into shadow, leaving a thin, long-limbed figure fleeing into the trees.

[Fenris](/characters/fenris) became a panther and pursued it through more illusions. The chase led to a clearing with a dark pond. A blue-haired water nymph rose from the water and sent watery tendrils toward them. An owlbear then emerged from the brush behind the party, trapping them between the two threats.
