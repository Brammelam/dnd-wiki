## Recap

### The Bounty Hunter's Bargain

The notes resumed with [Konstantin Carnelian](/characters/konstantin-carnelian) defeated but alive. He told [Varis](/characters/varis) and [Fenris](/characters/fenris) that [Lady Alabaster](/characters/lady-alabaster) had hired him over the death of her son Tom. He insisted that he had intended to capture them, not kill them.

A magical compass on [Konstantin Carnelian](/characters/konstantin-carnelian) pointed toward [Varis](/characters/varis), revealing how he had followed them. The pair took it and negotiated payment in exchange for enough healing to let the bounty hunter leave. He swore not to pursue them again and limped back to his horse.

### The Red Moon and Calen

Under a red moon, [Fenris](/characters/fenris) carved a flute from a bone taken from the dead water nymph. Its first music produced illusory spiders that played on [Varis](/characters/varis)'s fear. They interrupted the illusion, gathered herbs and recoverable arrows, divided the bounty hunter's gold, and rested. An attempt to identify the sword among their possessions revealed nothing beyond its balance.

The next morning they continued to [Calen](/places/calen), an elven settlement suffering from hunger. Rations were expensive. They paid [Rudolf the Great](/characters/rudolf-the-great) to identify their equipment and learned that the sword allowed its bearer to parry and counterattack when an enemy missed. The amulet contained a priest's blood and could frighten undead a limited number of times. The ring could whisper telepathically using words its wearer had stored, and the two compasses always pointed toward one another. The bow he examined was nonmagical.

[Varis](/characters/varis) attuned to the sword before the pair continued through dry farmland toward [Dior](/places/dior).

### Bob's News

The road entered [Dior](/places/dior) through a great mountain tunnel lined with dormant magical wards. Beyond it lay a circular lake, houses climbing the slopes, and a temple built out over the water.

[Bob Swords](/characters/bob-swords), an energetic halfling merchant with a gold tooth, brought them to his shop. He knew [Salander](/characters/salander), which helped establish trust. Over beer, he explained that [Helga](/characters/helga) was in town for her son's wedding to one of the Nine Bishops close to the cardinal. The marriage had political importance, and the ceremony was only days away.

[Bob Swords](/characters/bob-swords) suggested using the wedding's distractions to reach [Helga](/characters/helga). He would not intervene directly, but offered information and contacts. He also described an unofficial route to adventuring work outside the city's taxed guild system.

### Supplies and Preparations

[Fenris](/characters/fenris) sold his shield and scimitar. [Bob Swords](/characters/bob-swords) promised to show them two affordable sets of magical light armor the next morning and offered a contact who could help with the damaged bow. [Varis](/characters/varis) bought a larger quiver for thirty arrows, along with ordinary arrows and specially made arrows that struck harder.

With new equipment and a reason to watch the wedding, the pair began discussing allies, the whisper ring, and how to confront [Helga](/characters/helga) about their arrest.
