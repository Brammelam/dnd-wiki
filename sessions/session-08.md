## Recap

### Supplies and old comrades

After tending [Flint](/characters/flint) and [Peren](/characters/peren)'s wounds with a healer's kit, [Varis](/characters/varis) and [Elowen](/characters/elowen) bought supplies for the road to [Dior](/places/dior). [Varis](/characters/varis) purchased a dark green cloak from a seller of magical goods. When he considered an arrow advertised as thunder-blessed, [Elowen](/characters/elowen)'s *Detect Magic* confirmed that it was enchanted, and he bought it after haggling.

The same spell exposed a supposed vampiric dagger as a fake. The offended shopkeeper sent them away. [Varis](/characters/varis) later discovered that his cloak was also mundane, but kept it with the thought of reselling it elsewhere.

At [Rinden](/places/rinden)'s gates, [Markus](/characters/markus-lieutenant) recognized [Varis](/characters/varis) from military service. While they caught up, the guard explained that his unit was being sent to [Fort John](/places/fort-john) because of an uprising against its ruling noble. He invited his old comrade to visit. Outside the gates, refugees who had been refused entry gathered around priests distributing food. The travelers donated gold to the relief effort.

### Brad's delivery

[Brad](/characters/brad-academy-student) approached them with a locked metal box for [Professor Toltec](/characters/toltec) in [Dior](/places/dior). He would not disclose the contents, but claimed the box was harmless and that divination had shown it arriving safely in their hands. They accepted 40 gold in advance, with another 40 promised upon delivery.

[Varis](/characters/varis) also asked about fairy pollen he was carrying. [Brad](/characters/brad-academy-student) explained that it could be used in enchantments but required a skilled mage, leaving them to seek help with it in [Dior](/places/dior).

### The fisherman's house

The party continued along the coast after dark. [Elowen](/characters/elowen)'s magical lights made them easy to follow, and [Varis](/characters/varis) noticed figures behind them. She made him invisible so he could investigate, then approached a small house for shelter. When the frightened occupant refused entry, [Elowen](/characters/elowen) blasted the door open with *Eldritch Blast*.

Inside, [Elowen](/characters/elowen) caught the man's son trying to escape through a window. She used charm magic on the father, who became cooperative, but the boy remained terrified. Meanwhile, [Varis](/characters/varis) found six lightly armed pursuers, including a dwarf who appeared to lead them. Speaking from invisibility, he demanded to know what they wanted. The unseen voice frightened them into fleeing before they answered.

[Varis](/characters/varis) returned to the house. The party left a few gold pieces for the broken door and moved on, eventually leaving the still-charmed fisherman behind.

### A message at dawn

They stopped to rest at dawn. During meditation, [Elowen](/characters/elowen) saw a glowing blue woman in a sunlit field. The visitor introduced herself as [Sommerhimmel](/characters/sommerhimmel), a messenger of Queen Titania, and said that [Elowen](/characters/elowen)'s new powers were Titania's gift. A future task would be required in return, but its nature remained undisclosed.
