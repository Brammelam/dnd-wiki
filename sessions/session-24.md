## Recap

### Leaving Sire

Smoke still hung over the land when [Varis](/characters/varis) and [Fenris](/characters/fenris) returned to [Knut Pilsen](/characters/knut-pilsen) and his daughter [Anne Knutsen](/characters/anne-knutsen), soot-stained after the fires. Villagers knew little of fire-throwers in town; fear lingered where the forest had jumped the river. [Knut](/characters/knut-pilsen)'s arm remained burned, but he and his daughter were well enough to travel. They meant to try their luck in [Morgan's Keep](/places/morgans-keep) and thanked the pair.

[Varis](/characters/varis) showed the medallion recovered from the pyromancer in [Sire](/places/sire). The burned pages found with it contained the words "Temple of Life"; the pair also discussed the uncertain name Catherine or Katinka, neither of which the villagers recognized. At camp, [Varis](/characters/varis) meditated with the medallion and felt it strengthen his healing, though the surge of power was unsettling.

The pair decided to bring [Knut](/characters/knut-pilsen) and [Anne](/characters/anne-knutsen) along on the two-day journey toward [Morgan's Keep](/places/morgans-keep). They traveled with Roach and the farm horse Betty, sharing space as best they could. [Fenris](/characters/fenris) mused about one day commanding weather or flying as the farmlands rolled past. Passing [Dior](/places/dior), they noted a military camp already raised; soldiers moved along the road in good order.

### A livelihood for Knut

Approaching [Frontier](/places/frontier-outskirts) outside [Morgan's Keep](/places/morgans-keep), they found watchful guards and fewer civilians than usual. They took rooms at [The Final Straw](/places/the-final-straw), a converted farm inn that was busy that evening, and secured space for [Knut](/characters/knut-pilsen) and [Anne](/characters/anne-knutsen).

Over dinner, [Varis](/characters/varis) offered to buy Betty to give [Knut](/characters/knut-pilsen) money for a fresh start. The man said the horse was all he owned, but accepted that staying in the city would cost him. The pair paid for food and lodging, then agreed on thirty-five gold for Betty.

They discussed buying a small wagon and hiring someone to deliver goods and messages, tend the horses, fish, and cook for the camp. [Fenris](/characters/fenris) also mentioned farm work near the Grove with a half-elf named Eldar as another possibility for [Knut](/characters/knut-pilsen).

[Varis](/characters/varis) offered [Knut](/characters/knut-pilsen) the courier job the next morning; he asked a day or two to decide. The terms included food, shelter, a place for [Anne](/characters/anne-knutsen), and a silver a day. Their conversation turned to cartwright work and apprenticeship, then to [Varis](/characters/varis)'s military service: training in the capital, administrative work after he could no longer serve at the front, and the dismissal and imprisonment that followed. He remembered old comrades, including [Thor](/characters/thor-veteran).

They arranged to meet later at the Dark Horse in the [Common District](/places/common-district). [Fenris](/characters/fenris) would price horses and speak to Ragngrim, a dwarf smith he knew. Hans, a cartwright, offered a repaired wagon for forty coins, and they arranged the exchange and delivery.

### Old contacts

[Varis](/characters/varis) crossed to the Heaven Port district and the Medallion inn, hoping to find [Thor](/characters/thor-veteran). His old comrade arrived leaning on a staff, with a leg injury that had changed him considerably since their service together.

[Fenris](/characters/fenris) met an old acquaintance who had turned to selling information after a basilisk injured him on a northern expedition. Their conversation raised the [Humanus Association](/factions/humanus-association) and a group called the Horses as possible leads, but produced no firm answers. They arranged another meeting in four hours. Meanwhile, [Varis](/characters/varis) followed [Thor](/characters/thor-veteran) through the alleys and covered his presence with casual conversation when the veteran emerged from a doorway.

[Varis](/characters/varis) pursued leads on [Lieutenant Jet](/characters/lieutenant-jet)'s wife in the Hole, considered whether the cardinal's truth magic might help, and spoke with dwarf artificers who sold their services locally. [Fenris](/characters/fenris) found two possible cheese buyers and planned to send [Knut](/characters/knut-pilsen) for stock. He proposed a camp south of the city near the coast and gave [Knut](/characters/knut-pilsen) four gold for a fishing rod and bait. They also discussed keeping in touch telepathically while making their separate arrangements.

Before the evening meeting, [Varis](/characters/varis) bought cloth to cover his equipment. [Thor](/characters/thor-veteran) returned with his superior [Magda](/characters/magda), a one-armed half-orc who worked in [General Kamp's intelligence network](/factions/third-army).

### The crypt plan

The intelligence service offered to help if [Thor](/characters/thor-veteran) vouched for them. Its representative connected [Lieutenant Jet](/characters/lieutenant-jet)'s role in the investigation with his wife's debt to the [Humanus Association](/factions/humanus-association). To expose the people behind the [Crown Prince murder](/events/crown-prince-murder) accusation, the party would need to infiltrate a meeting of the association's inner circle.

The service offered false identities, disguises, and a ring with limited power to question the recently dead. [Gregory Alabaster](/characters/gregory-alabaster), a deceased member of the association's twelve-person circle, lay in the [Crypt of Alabaster](/places/crypt-of-alabaster) beneath the cathedral and might know where the group would meet. [Fenris](/characters/fenris) explained that his own magic dealt with plants and animals rather than the dead. [Varis](/characters/varis) also offered the burned papers and medallion associated with [Brad](/characters/brad-academy-student) for examination. They agreed to enter the crypt next, then returned to their camp arrangements and preparations.
