## Recap

The party left [Session 25](/sessions/session-25) with [Gregory Alabaster the Fourth](/characters/gregory-alabaster)'s cobalt orb in shards. Undead still poured from the crypt—including a risen corpse among them that might have been the [murdered Crown Prince](/events/crown-prince-murder), the same death that had framed the party. They could not explain what the orb had done or who had bound [Gregory](/characters/gregory-alabaster) with it.

### Crypt stairs

[Fenris](/characters/fenris) forced the upper crypt doors open and found the passage packed with shamblers. He cast *Wind Wall* upward. Every wax taper blew out; the corridor went dark, and the gust slammed the doors shut behind them.

[Varis](/characters/varis) carried [Peren](/characters/peren), who was bleeding and half-conscious, and swept the [Amulet of the Living](/characters/varis#notable-equipment) in wide arcs. The dead hung back as if turned by a cleric's prayer. Farther down the passage, pale blue eyes watched from the crowd.

Before the party sealed the upper exit, [Varis](/characters/varis) shot one shambler in the head. That corpse's glow dimmed; one eye went dark. They wrestled the door closed and climbed the stairs while something hammered the panel from below. A blow glanced [Fenris](/characters/fenris). Another zombie's sores burst, splashing him with foul bile.

[Fenris](/characters/fenris) drew the Blade of the Ethereal. He and one attacker blinked onto the Ethereal Plane long enough for [Fenris](/characters/fenris) to block a swing, take a hit that dropped him prone, and endure a stomp before the sword pulled both of them back to the stairwell. [Varis](/characters/varis) gave [Peren](/characters/peren) another healing potion until the rogue could stand, barely.

### Nave

Noise from the nave drew parishioners toward the crypt stairs. A huge slime creature tore the stair-door away and threw [Fenris](/characters/fenris) into a pew, still covered in crypt filth. [Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) stepped aside with a polite nod, then turned guards toward the undead pressing up from below.

[Varis](/characters/varis) carried [Peren](/characters/peren) toward the rear sacristy and persuaded a frightened monk to help. [Jamaris Jamarisen Llamar](/characters/jamaris-jamarisen-llamar) charged down the main stairs and drove his spear's butt into the slime giant, knocking it back onto the shamblers behind—a short reprieve.

[Fenris](/characters/fenris) called lightning down through the nave. Bolts tore through undead ranks while [Varis](/characters/varis) picked off glowing eyes. Then the crowd screamed: when [Varis](/characters/varis) dropped another armored corpse, witnesses thought it was the [Crown Prince](/events/crown-prince-murder). Panic spread. [Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) shouldered past three guards. [Fenris](/characters/fenris) traded stares with the head celebrant while an older priest dropped his staff in the crush.

[Varis](/characters/varis) tried to quiet the herald; a soldier knocked him aside. The senior priest knelt and prayed. Light punched up through the stained glass and hammered the undead back. [Fenris](/characters/fenris), still fighting, shouted *"Humanus!"* and *"Ilmater, strike down your enemies!"* as another lightning bolt shattered stone on the stairs. [Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) stood over a pile of fallen watchmen.

[Jamaris](/characters/jamaris-jamarisen-llamar) shouted "Stop!" with mock authority. Lamps died. The holy light that had pinned the dead faltered; corpses froze for a breath, then stirred again. Only one guard obeyed him.

[Varis](/characters/varis) mapped exits: side doors, a limping sentinel, a thief clinging to another guard as if nothing were wrong. He told everyone to scatter. [Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) banged helmets together, took a missed swing, and allies pommeled an elder cleric unconscious without killing him. When light returned, the horde lurched forward. The party ran through the plaza past tent refugees.

### Common District

In the street, a woman pointed at them and watchmen gave chase. [Fenris](/characters/fenris) raised roots from the cobbles with *Entangle*, catching accuser and soldier alike while someone hissed threats about snitches. The party slipped south into the [Common District](/places/common-district), dodged a patrol, and collapsed into a dim tavern. A boy brought wash water; [Fenris](/characters/fenris) still stank of crypt gore.

Over quiet drinks they compared notes: [Humanus Association](/factions/humanus-association) propaganda still blamed them for the [Crown Prince murder](/events/crown-prince-murder). [Gregory](/characters/gregory-alabaster) had named [Lady Alabaster](/characters/lady-alabaster)'s wine cellar for the next meeting, roughly ninety minutes away. They planned to take a member's cloak and use an alchemy draught that might pass [Varis](/characters/varis) for human for about an hour. He would enter because he knew the surroundings best. [Jamaris Jamarisen Llamar](/characters/jamaris-jamarisen-llamar) introduced himself to the group and said his faith had brought him there.

[Thor](/characters/thor-veteran), leaning on a bad leg, beckoned from the alley. They followed him to a coach; doors shut, hooves clopped toward [Lady Alabaster](/characters/lady-alabaster)'s grounds as dusk fell.

### After the coach

They still owed [Maurus](/characters/maurus) answers while pamphlets called them kingslayers. They also agreed to ask [the Academy](/factions/the-academy) about the blue orb once they had time. For now, the party tried to stop a finely dressed man heading to [Lady Alabaster](/characters/lady-alabaster)'s house and find out whether he was connected to the accusations.

[Jamaris](/characters/jamaris-jamarisen-llamar)'s drunken ruse failed, and his target ran. [Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) emerged from a thorn bush and struck the man with the flat of her glass axe, knocking him unconscious. [Varis](/characters/varis) broke a nearby window with a stone to distract the resident while the others carried their prisoner away. Searching him produced one gold coin and a bag with a torn seam, though they could not establish what it had contained.

Their prisoner was alive. The party postponed the plan to impersonate nobles and weighed the time needed to recover against the risk of missing the meeting. [Fenris](/characters/fenris) considered using an animal form, but they waited before committing to their next move.
