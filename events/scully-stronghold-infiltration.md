The **Scully Stronghold infiltration** was the party's opening move against a sealed noble redoubt off [Rinden](/places/rinden): [Lady Amber](/characters/lady-amber)'s invitations controlled the ferry, and the group used invisibility, Wild Shape, and social cover to board.

## Approach

In Rinden's **Old Town** docks, the party watched the **lektør** barge — powered by donkeys walking in circles — that carried cargo and invited guests to the island fortress. [Elowen](/characters/elowen) approached a mage she knew, **[Brad](/characters/brad-academy-student)**, spun a tale of searching for a lost love on the stronghold, and secured passage as his guest. [Varis](/characters/varis) was made **invisible** and slipped aboard; [Fenris](/characters/fenris) held back to enter later as a small animal among cargo; [Flint](/characters/flint) remained in Rinden as backup.

## Landing

After a short crossing, the barge docked at the stronghold. [Elowen](/characters/elowen) went ashore as a guest while [Varis](/characters/varis), still unseen, scouted the dock, outbuildings, and path toward the main gate.

## Inside the stronghold

[Elowen](/characters/elowen) left her fish familiar beneath the dock to watch the traffic. Inside, [Varis](/characters/varis) overheard a priest of the [Church of the Triad](/factions/temple-of-tyr) asking [Lady Amber](/characters/lady-amber) to help Rinden's poor. She distrusted the church but agreed to consider his request. [Brad](/characters/brad-academy-student) then secured two hundred gold from her to investigate a lead concerning her missing sister.

[Flint](/characters/flint) distracted the dock guards and threw [Fenris](/characters/fenris), in mouse form, aboard the barge. A cat's attack broke the transformation, but Fenris persuaded it to leave him alone and found sourleaf hidden beneath the wine in a barrel. He kept a sample. Flint was detained after [Father Terry](/characters/father-terry) accused him of attacking a woman; Old Beate's confused account led to his release.

## Outcome

The party brought the sourleaf to [Salander](/characters/salander), who identified its origin as Darukesh. He paid five gold each and directed them to [Helga](/characters/helga), then in [Dior](/places/dior) for her daughter's wedding. He also recommended [Bob Swords](/characters/bob-swords) as a local contact. The notes establish contraband on the barge, but do not establish who controlled the smuggling.
