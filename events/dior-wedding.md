The wedding in [Dior](/places/dior) gave the party a chance to confront [Helga](/characters/helga), the [King's Guard](/factions/kings-guard) captain who had arrested them. Surveillance and threats led to a meeting at [Tyr Moir](/places/tyr-moir) and a new lead in the [Crown Prince murder](/events/crown-prince-murder) investigation.

## Recap

### The Wedding

[Helga](/characters/helga)'s son was marrying one of the Nine Bishops close to the cardinal. She oversaw security and preparations at the temple in [Dior](/places/dior)'s lake. [Bob Swords](/characters/bob-swords) told [Varis](/characters/varis) and [Fenris](/characters/fenris) about the wedding in [Session 15](/sessions/session-15), suggesting that the ceremony might distract her guards.

After the [crypt expedition](/events/dior-crypt), the party considered an attack from the gallery with fire arrows and blocked exits. By [Session 19](/sessions/session-19), they were looking for quieter ways to obtain answers.

### Reconnaissance — Session 19

[Fenris](/characters/fenris) entered the temple as a cat and knocked over a vase, allowing [Peren](/characters/peren) to slip past the guards. The party explored storage and training rooms below the waterline, decorated prayer rooms upstairs, and an instrument store. Some doors remained locked. [Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) surveyed from above while [Varis](/characters/varis) kept watch.

The party followed [Helga](/characters/helga) to a workshop and a guarded manor and watched her meet an unidentified woman on a terrace. Attempts to find an entrance through the manor's defenses failed. Their damaged compass also required attention before they could use it for tracking.

[Bob Swords](/characters/bob-swords) supplied elemental arrows and a grappling hook and promised to investigate temple plans and drainage. At Nymph's Tears, the party bought healing and laxative potions. They considered sleep medicine, an anonymous threat, eavesdropping, and compromising the bridegroom, but did not settle on those tactics.

### The Invitation and Tracker — Session 20

The party decided to draw [Helga](/characters/helga) to a private meeting at [Tyr Moir](/places/tyr-moir). They retained a wedding fallback with invitations from [Bob Swords](/characters/bob-swords), clothes from the Blue Cloak, and cosmetics for disguise.

[Peren](/characters/peren) used the flute to create illusory rats, scattering a dog and the workers near the temple entrance. [Varis](/characters/varis) then used the whisper ring to threaten [Helga](/characters/helga): meet alone at the graveyard at dusk, or the wedding would be ruined. Her guards searched and increased security.

Later, [Fenris](/characters/fenris) approached in cat form while chasing a mouse and slipped a compass into [Helga](/characters/helga)'s belt pouch. [Peren](/characters/peren) used its partner to follow her movements. The notes do not explain the repair of the compass described as damaged in the previous session.

### The Graveyard Opening — Session 20

[Peren](/characters/peren) attacked a lone guard watching the chapel. The guard briefly pinned him before he reversed the hold and disarmed the man. [Varis](/characters/varis) arrived with his bow, and the party subdued the soldier without killing him. [Fenris](/characters/fenris) helped bind, gag, and hide him.

[Helga](/characters/helga) entered the graveyard alone at dusk, set her lantern beside the central statue, and waited. [Varis](/characters/varis) shot out the light and descended from a rooftop. [Peren](/characters/peren) appeared among the monuments, and [Fenris](/characters/fenris) stepped forward at the gate.

### The Interrogation — Session 21

The next session recorded [Varis](/characters/varis), [Fenris](/characters/fenris), and [Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) questioning [Helga](/characters/helga). She remained suspicious but pointed them toward [Lieutenant Jet](/characters/lieutenant-jet), who had filed the report. She suspected pressure through his wife's debts and the [Humanus Association](/factions/humanus-association). His wife was imprisoned.

[Helga](/characters/helga) offered to reduce official attention through her contacts if the party kept a low profile. She refused public involvement and warned that any further approach would force her to arrest them. She also mentioned the cardinal's power to compel truth at the King's Council.

[Varis](/characters/varis) raised his bow, but [Fenris](/characters/fenris) calmed the exchange. Further threats produced no more information. The party released the captive guard and left.

### Aftermath

[Bob Swords](/characters/bob-swords) bought back the invitations at a reduced price. The party discussed freeing [Lieutenant Jet](/characters/lieutenant-jet)'s wife to obtain information or use her as a hostage. Outside [Dior](/places/dior), they found soldiers preparing to march and smoke rising to the west.

The subsequent journeys took [Fenris](/characters/fenris) and [Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) toward [Sire](/places/sire) in [Session 22](/sessions/session-22), and [Varis](/characters/varis) and [Peren](/characters/peren) toward [Morgan's Keep](/places/morgans-keep) in [Session 23](/sessions/session-23).
