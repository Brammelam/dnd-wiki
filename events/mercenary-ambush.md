The **Mercenary Ambush** occurred when [Fat Hans](/characters/fat-hans) and his hired fighters attacked the party on the road.

## Background

[Fat Hans](/characters/fat-hans), a former prison guard, claimed that some of the wall guards killed during the [escape from Morgan's Keep](/events/escape-from-morgans-keep) were sons of a countess. According to him, she had hired his mercenaries to avenge them.

## The Ambush

After a cart crash on the road, the party fought and defeated [Fat Hans](/characters/fat-hans)'s mercenaries. They learned his account of the pursuit while interrogating him afterward.

## Outcome

The party took a horse, a compass, and other equipment. They tied [Fat Hans](/characters/fat-hans) and the surviving mercenaries to a tree before continuing toward the next town.

## The Countess's Identity

The employer's identity remained uncertain. In [Session 7](/sessions/session-07), [Salander](/characters/salander) could find no noble called "Countess Barros." The notes do not establish whether the compass taken here was related to the later tracking compasses.
