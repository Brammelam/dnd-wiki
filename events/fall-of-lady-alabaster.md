## Summary

The party infiltrated a masked [Humanus Association](/factions/humanus-association) meeting beneath [Lady Alabaster](/characters/lady-alabaster)'s estate and heard its members discuss prisoners, raids against elves, and using the cathedral undead crisis for political gain. [Varis](/characters/varis) glimpsed a woman behind a slipping mask and believed she was [Lady Alabaster](/characters/lady-alabaster). The party returned to [Thor](/characters/thor-veteran) with leads, but still needed a confession or firmer evidence to clear their names.

After [Thor](/characters/thor-veteran)'s allies promised access to the King's Council, the party intercepted [Lady Alabaster](/characters/lady-alabaster) aboard a departing ship. Political risk kept those allies from making the arrest themselves. The fight set the vessel ablaze and revealed imprisoned elves below deck. [Varis](/characters/varis) also recognized a half-orc captive with green marks on her neck from an earlier encounter with [Elowen](/characters/elowen).

They freed the prisoners while the ship flooded. [Peren](/characters/peren) tried to help [Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) in the river, but their rope caught on an anchor. [Fenris](/characters/fenris) hauled the anchor and swimmers toward shore, then returned to free [Peren](/characters/peren) from a submerged window and revive him. With [Lady Alabaster](/characters/lady-alabaster) alive and bound, the party brought her toward the palace at the head of a crowd demanding a hearing.

Before the council, the cardinal of the [Church of the Triad](/factions/temple-of-tyr) compelled [Lady Alabaster](/characters/lady-alabaster) to tell the truth. She admitted arranging the party's false accusation in the [Crown Prince murder](/events/crown-prince-murder) and named [Old Garreth](/factions/toll-guild) as the person who ordered her. [Viktor Carnelian](/characters/viktor-carnelian) then attacked her. The cardinal intercepted his spear and was wounded in the chest; after [Fenris](/characters/fenris) partially healed her, another strike pierced her throat.

The fighting continued in [Session 30](/sessions/session-30). [Helga](/characters/helga) led the king toward a door behind the throne while guards turned on one another. An unnamed scarred giant carried the cardinal out with her blind priestess still healing her, while the cardinal's dwarf warrior covered their retreat. General Kamp of the [Third Army](/factions/third-army) warned [Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) to run, then withdrew with [Gunther](/characters/gunther) and [Molly Alabaster](/characters/molly-alabaster). Before leaving, the mage stole an amulet hanging on the wall in the king's room, a separate item from the [Amulet of the Living](/characters/varis#notable-equipment) carried by [Varis](/characters/varis).

[Varis](/characters/varis) brought [Viktor](/characters/viktor-carnelian) down with a magical ice arrow, but his glowing armor restored him enough to rise and withdraw. [Old Garreth](/factions/toll-guild) left despite [Peren](/characters/peren)'s flash bomb, and [Konstantin Carnelian](/characters/konstantin-carnelian) followed his brother after telling [Fenris](/characters/fenris) they could go. [Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) severed [Lady Alabaster](/characters/lady-alabaster)'s lower leg and took her necklace, leaving her alive and bleeding. As the others withdrew, [Peren](/characters/peren) pursued [Viktor](/characters/viktor-carnelian) into the palace corridor. The cardinal's survival, the party's legal standing, and the prince's murder remained unresolved.
