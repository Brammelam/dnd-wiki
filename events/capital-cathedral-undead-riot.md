## Summary

After [Varis](/characters/varis) shattered the cobalt orb on [Gregory Alabaster the Fourth](/characters/gregory-alabaster) in [Session 25](/sessions/session-25), undead surged through the cathedral crypt and nave—including a risen corpse the crowd took for the [murdered Crown Prince](/events/crown-prince-murder). On the crypt stairs [Varis](/characters/varis) carried the wounded [Peren](/characters/peren) and used the [Amulet of the Living](/characters/varis#notable-equipment) to hold shamblers back; [Fenris](/characters/fenris) fought through *Wind Wall* blackouts, bile-soaked close quarters, and jumps with the Blade of the Ethereal before a slime giant and a full nave riot. [Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) redirected guards; [Jamaris Jamarisen Llamar](/characters/jamaris-jamarisen-llamar)'s counterfeit *"Stop!"* bought seconds; clergy holy light and Fenris's storm magic clashed in the chaos. The party fled into the [Common District](/places/common-district), planned a disguise for the [Humanus Association](/factions/humanus-association) cellar meet at [Lady Alabaster](/characters/lady-alabaster)'s estate, and left with [Thor](/characters/thor-veteran) in a coach.
