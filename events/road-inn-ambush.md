The road inn ambush was [Thorn](/characters/thorn-alchemist)'s attempt to settle a grudge from [Session 9](/sessions/session-09). After the [serpent quest](/events/thorn-serpent-and-visions), [Elowen](/characters/elowen) had destroyed his door in anger over his sleeping potion on the oars, which was meant to leave [Peren](/characters/peren) alone against the sea serpent. [Thorn](/characters/thorn-alchemist) later caught up with [Varis](/characters/varis) and [Peren](/characters/peren) on the road from [Dior](/places/dior).

## Setup

[Thorn](/characters/thorn-alchemist) posed as a traveling herbalist at the inn where the pair slept in the stable. He drugged their drinks with a sleep compound. Earlier that day, they had discussed brewing with fey pollen and surbla leaf. [Peren](/characters/peren) ruined the surbla mixture by throwing a reagent into the fire, but commissioned a fairy healing potion and learned enough to prepare a basic healing mixture himself.

## Fight

[Thorn](/characters/thorn-alchemist) attacked alone. When [Peren](/characters/peren) woke groggy in the stable, the alchemist stabbed him in the back. [Peren](/characters/peren) fought back and pinned him while [Varis](/characters/varis) joined the melee. The brawl left [Thorn](/characters/thorn-alchemist) badly wounded and the horses frightened.

When [Peren](/characters/peren) tried to calm a horse, dizziness overcame him and he collapsed against a stall door. [Varis](/characters/varis) checked his breathing and laid him on his side until he began to recover.

## Aftermath

[Varis](/characters/varis) gave [Thorn](/characters/thorn-alchemist) a healing potion to stabilize him. Under questioning, the alchemist admitted using a sleeping mixture and tried to justify his retaliation for the damage to his home. [Peren](/characters/peren) kept his poisoning notebook and some daggers. After showing their credentials and being asked to leave, the pair continued with [Lynn Salt](/characters/lynn-salt)'s wagon toward [Morgan's Keep](/places/morgans-keep).
