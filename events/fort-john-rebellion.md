The **Fort John rebellion** was a regional crisis sparked by **[Karl Marsensen](/characters/karl-marsensen)**'s arrest. Rebels held the fort; **[Baron Peridott](/characters/baron-peridott)** and **[Markus](/characters/markus-lieutenant)** hired the party as independent agents to extract Karl or resolve the standoff.

## Portal echo (Mirbog)

[Elowen](/characters/elowen) met [Mirbog](/characters/mirbog), a guardian sent by Titania to keep the portal sealed. Their encounter concerned the relics removed during the [Lake Hydra Hunt](/events/lake-hydra-hunt): [Fenris](/characters/fenris) held Blade of the Ethereal, while Elowen held Sprout and Nathair's Mask. After escaping the crystal chamber, Mirbog supplied a formula for restoring the seal. The party feared that taking all three relics could release the dead, but did not return them ([Session 12](/sessions/session-12)).

## Inside the fort

[Elowen](/characters/elowen) used her fish familiar to place *Stinking Cloud* at the fort's well. [Fenris](/characters/fenris) then became a giant spider to draw the defenders away from a side door. [Varis](/characters/varis) and [Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) entered, subdued a guard, and staged a defense against the spider. [Frida Marsensen](/characters/frida-marsensen) welcomed them as allies. During the diversion, [Sommerhimmel](/characters/sommerhimmel) confronted [Elowen](/characters/elowen) over the stolen relics and took her away.

Squirrel scouting later left [Fenris](/characters/fenris) in the hands of two men inside the tower. He escaped with *Entangle* and *Wind Wall*, then hid in the well. [Frida Marsensen](/characters/frida-marsensen) recognized the druid's interference and increased the guard. Meanwhile, [Varis](/characters/varis) and [Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) learned about hunger and reluctant conscription in the beer cellar. They reached the captive [Karl Marsensen](/characters/karl-marsensen), who offered a reward for freedom and a chance to negotiate food supplies before winter ([Session 13](/sessions/session-13)).

## Sabotage and settlement

[Varis](/characters/varis) punctured the beer barrels while [Fenris](/characters/fenris) made the well water look and smell poisoned. The notes describe magical changes to the water, not confirmed poisoning. The party escaped the fort and reported to [Baron Peridott](/characters/baron-peridott).

The first talks with [Frida Marsensen](/characters/frida-marsensen) failed, and a rider escaped toward [Dior](/places/dior) to seek help. After thirsty townspeople began deserting, the two sides agreed to exchange limited rations for surrender of the fort ([Session 14](/sessions/session-14)).

## Aftermath

[Markus](/characters/markus-lieutenant) helped the party avoid [Konstantin Carnelian](/characters/konstantin-carnelian), who sought them on behalf of [Lady Alabaster](/characters/lady-alabaster). The baron paid thirteen gold each, and [Varis](/characters/varis) chose a finely balanced nonmagical bow. A lieutenant also referred [Gal'Rok](/characters/galrok) to [Thor](/characters/thor-veteran), an intelligence contact in [Morgan's Keep](/places/morgans-keep). The party loaded [Brad](/characters/brad-academy-student)'s metal box onto their horse and continued toward [Dior](/places/dior), where [Helga](/characters/helga) was staying.
